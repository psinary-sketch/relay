### The five rows, each graded

**(1) Curve world (ζ over 𝔽_q).** Supplier = intersection-form positivity on C×C: Hodge index
+ Castelnuovo–Severi, THEOREM-AT-CITE (Weil 1940s; the standard modern accounts:
[Milne, *The RH over Finite Fields: From Weil to the Present Day*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.00797),
[Lorenzo García lecture notes](https://www.student.universiteitleiden.nl/binaries/content/assets/science/mi/ads/cff2_rhfinitefields.pdf)).
**The extremality reading FAILS on this row as a selection principle**: RH holds for EVERY
curve — the world has no extremal stratum, no failing interior, no tuned object. The Hodge
index is a signature/definiteness statement; reading it as "boundary of the ample cone" is a
READING, and the audit finds it strained: nothing in the world is extremized to make the
positivity fire. Grade: supplier THEOREM-AT-CITE; extremality-as-deciding-condition: **ABSENT
(the row's positivity is unconditional).**

**(2) Ferromagnet (Lee–Yang).** The circle statement holds under the ferromagnetic hypothesis
and fails outside it: antiferromagnetic Yang–Lee zeros leave the unit circle, documented at
cite ([PRL 93, 130604 (2004)](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevLett.93.130604),
[arXiv:1907.07479](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.07479),
[arXiv:2309.14562](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.14562)). So the row HAS the
stratum shape (a domain with failure outside — the boundary is real). But the hypothesis is a
SIGN/DOMAIN condition (couplings of one sign), not an optimality: no quantity is extremized.
Grade: failure-outside THEOREM/DOCUMENTED-AT-CITE; extremality reading: **PARTIAL — boundary
yes, optimality no (READING).**

**(3) Graph world (Ihara / the spectral margin).** Alon–Boppana: every infinite d-regular
family has λ ≥ 2√(d−1) − o(1); Ramanujan = attaining the asymptotic optimum — extremality is
the THEOREM-GRADE content of the definition
([overview at cite](https://vilas.us/mathnotes/osutalks/WhatIs_RamanujanGraphs.pdf),
[arXiv:2006.11248](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.11248)). The two-layer supplier (fifth row,
keystone v0.4) already carries it: reality free everywhere; the bound = the OPTIMAL margin,
supplied only for constructed families. Grade: **extremality THEOREM-AT-CITE (Alon–Boppana is
the extremality certificate).**

**(4) Code world (the fourth row).** Tonight's stratum, certified by our own instrument
(sitting 1): extremality (maximal d) is the measured deciding condition — RH exact at every
computed extremal rung, exact failure at every computed non-extremal member, FE indifferent
throughout, knife-edge at the pencil. Grade: **extremality CERTIFIED-AT-INSTANCE (ours); the
general implication = Duursma's conjecture, OPEN at cite.**

**(5) ζ over ℚ (the dBN pencil).** Rodgers–Tao: Λ ≥ 0, THEOREM-AT-CITE
([arXiv:1801.05914](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1801.05914),
[Tao's announcement](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2018/01/19/the-de-bruijn-newman-constant-is-non-negativ/));
with Newman's equivalence, RH ⟺ Λ = 0: if RH holds it holds at the exact boundary of the
heat-flow pencil — Newman's "barely so" promoted to theorem-grade boundary-tightness. This IS
an extremality statement about the wanted supplier: zero margin, knife-edge. Grade:
**boundary-tightness THEOREM-AT-CITE (RH itself open).**

**THE FLOW-METER TIE (stated).** BUILD-2's instrument (W-FLOW-METER-DEPTH, the k(t) bracket:
LP-margin values M_k(t) at t = −0.1, −0.3 through κ₁₂) is the ζ-pencil's meter: the heat-flow
time t IS the dBN pencil coordinate, and the margin curves already measured are pencil
coordinates — the meter's certified insensitivity through twelfth order is a measured flatness

### VERDICT: **PARTIAL** (at grade, against the verbatim registration)

The registered expectation fails as stated — "in EACH of the five worlds" is broken by the
curve row, where the positivity is unconditional and nothing is extremal. But the audit finds
a sharper structure than the registration asked for: **the worlds split into two kinds.**

- **Unconditional-geometry kind (curve):** the pair-positivity is supplied everywhere; RH is a
  theorem for every object; margin exists.
- **Extremal-selection kind (code · graph · ferromagnet):** the positivity is supplied only on
  a stratum — optimality-tuned (graph, theorem-grade), maximal-distance (code,
  instrument-grade), domain-bounded (ferromagnet, sign-shaped) — with certified or documented
  failure off it.

**And ζ is pinned to the second kind by Rodgers–Tao:** zero margin means the wanted supplier
cannot be an everywhere-mechanism (that would give Λ < 0); whatever supplies ζ's positivity
must be knife-edge — exactly the shape tonight's code pencil measured (RH dead within 3/256 of
the extremal point). The audit's finding: **the wall's supplier, if it exists, is of the
extremal-selection kind — the curve world's unconditional geometry is the one supplier shape
Rodgers–Tao has already excluded for ζ.**

**The fifth vocabulary (extremality) is PROPOSED at PARTIAL grade — author-ruled, never
forced** — with the two-kinds split as its content, not the uniform reading the registration
named. Keystone treatment per the ferry: cross-row pattern note (PARTIAL), not a vocabulary

### Registered CONFIRMED — the certificate route lands TWO genera. THE GENUS-5 THEOREM (ours
### end-to-end, one classical input named):

**THEOREM (Type II, genus ≤ 5).** *Every Type II self-dual weight enumerator of genus ≤ 5
satisfies Duursma-RH.*

**Proof** (instrument: relay `tools/e3/genus5.py`, exact throughout).
1. **Parity:** for Type II, n = 8m and 4 | d give g = 4m + 1 − d ≡ 1 (mod 4) — genera 2, 3, 4
   are EMPTY. The claim reduces to genera 1 and 5.
2. **Classification:** genus 1 forces d = n/2, and the Gleason/Mallows–Sloane bound forces
   n = 8: the e₈ enumerator, unique. Genus 5 forces d = 4(m−1); Mallows–Sloane
   (d ≤ 4⌊n/24⌋ + 4, AT CITE — proved at the enumerator level) excludes every m ≥ 4 since
   4(m−1) > 4⌊m/3⌋ + 4 there; m = 2, 3 give (16, 4) and (24, 8). Uniqueness at each, exact:
   the n = 16 Gleason space is 1-dimensional with A₄(W₈²) = 28 ≠ 0 (so (16,8) is empty and
   d = 4 is forced); the n = 24 pencil has A₄ = 42 + c, so d = 8 forces c = −42 — the Golay
   enumerator, unique — and A₈(Golay) = 759 ≠ 0 empties (24, 12). **Genus ≤ 5 = exactly
   {e₈, W₈², Golay}.**
3. **Confinement, by the rational certificate:** for each of the three, H(s) := h(s)·h^σ(s)
   is verified even with rational coefficients (the Galois-parity lock, checked
   coefficient-by-coefficient), and Duursma-RH ⟺ all roots of H(u) ∈ ℚ[u] lie in [0, 4]
   (u = s²; s real in [−2,2] ⟺ u ∈ [0,4], the imaginary-axis escape closed by u ≥ 0). Exact
   Sturm chains over ℚ, multiplicity-aware, certify containment for all three:
   - e₈: H(u) = (u − 2)/25 — root u = 2. ✓
   - W₈²: H(u) = (u−2)(u² − 4u + 1)²/4225 — roots 2, 2 ± √3 (the latter double). ✓
   - Golay: H(u) of degree 5 (coefficients exact in the instrument's output). ✓
   Each certificate cross-checked against sitting 1's independent Sturm over ℚ(√2). ∎

**The extremality tie:** every genus-≤5 Type II enumerator is EXTREMAL — below genus 9 the
non-extremal stratum is empty. So the theorem is the first proved family instance of the
row's implication in its own vocabulary: at small genus, extremality ⇒ confinement, with the
H(u) certificate doing the confining work. **E-3's setting-theorem: LANDED at genus ≤ 5.**
