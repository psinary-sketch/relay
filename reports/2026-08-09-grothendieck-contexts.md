## §1 — E1: **CONFIRMED — NATIVE-ADDITIVE-DOOR-SHUT**

*"semirings of **characteristic one**"*, where *"the multiplicative unit 1 is **idempotent for the
addition** and fulfills 1 ⊕ 1 = 1"*, with `Zmax = (Z ∪ {−∞}, max, +)` — and `max(a, a) = a` for
**every** element, so the additive monoid is idempotent throughout, not just at 1.

**The argument, in full:**

> Let `(M, ⊕)` be a commutative monoid with `a ⊕ a = a` for all `a`. Let `ι : M → K(M)` be the
> Grothendieck group completion. In the **group** `K(M)`, `ι(a) + ι(a) = ι(a ⊕ a) = ι(a)`.
> Subtracting `ι(a)` — legal, because `K(M)` is a group — gives **`ι(a) = 0`**. Since `K(M)` is
> generated by `ι(M)`, **`K(M) = 0`.**

**Every element dies. The additive Grothendieck completion of any idempotent monoid is trivial.**

> **VERDICT: NATIVE-ADDITIVE-DOOR-SHUT. No signed form arises by completing ⊕ — not for this square,
> and not for any tropical/characteristic-one candidate. This is a structural constraint on the
> WHOLE CANDIDATE CLASS, not on one construction.**

## §3 — E3: **REFUTED — the subtle feature is not about reduction**

I registered it as concerning *reduction / cancellativity*. **Read in full, it is not:**

> *"A subtle feature appears with respect to the expected composition rule **Ψ(β) ∘ Ψ(β′) =
> Ψ(ββ′)**. In fact, the equality holds when ββ′ ∈ Q but **when one composes Ψ(β) ∘ Ψ(β′) for
> irrational value of β and with ββ′ ∈ Q, one obtains the TANGENTIAL DEFORMATION of the
> correspondence Ψ(ββ′)**. The reason behind this result is that for irrational β, the Frobenius
> correspondence Ψ(β) has a **flexibility** which is automatically inherited by composition…"*

irrational parameters.**

**AND THIS BEARS ON THE DOOR QUESTION MORE THAN THE REGISTERED READING WOULD HAVE.** A trace on a

where rational products meet irrational parameters is the kind of structure that obstructs a naive
trace** — which is where their arrest sits. **Stated as an observation about the shape of their
difficulty, not as a claim about its resolution.**
