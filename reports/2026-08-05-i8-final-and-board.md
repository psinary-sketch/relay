## §1 — I-8 REACHES ITS FINAL FORM AND STOPS REFINING

**THE CRITERION — FINDABILITY FROM THE MENTION.** A term is **held** when its statement is
**co-located with the mention, or explicitly pointed at from it.** Not when its name appears; not
when its name appears often; and not when its content words appear somewhere else in the corpus.
**The unit of the test is the reader's single step from a name to the thing it names.**

**The two failed proxies, recorded as the path to it so neither is tried again:**

| proxy | how it over-credits | the case that killed it |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **name-count** | **repetition is unresolvability's signature, not its refutation** — a stated term is stated once and pointed at thereafter, while an unstated one must be re-named at every use | Mallows–Sloane 8× · Gleason 8× · Hankel-ratio 6×, **content zero in all three**, all three named premises of landed rows |
| **content-words-anywhere** | matches **generic phrasing in unrelated files**, crediting the corpus for containing the words rather than the reader for finding them | `non-discriminating by design` passed the automated screen and is, at its point of use, a verdict label whose criterion is not stated there |

**Both over-credit, in opposite directions, and the criterion is what remains when both failures
are excluded: a screen measures the reader's step, not the corpus's word inventory.**

**I-8 is now a criterion rather than an approximation.** Neither proxy is to be re-adopted as a
shortcut; an automated pass may propose candidates, but its verdicts are advisory and must be
closed by hand. **Further refinement is not authorized without a case I-8 fails on real corpus
material** — the reopening condition is stated so the closure is not permanent by default.

## §2 — THE META-WORK STOPPING RULE (loom, standing)

> **A check whose yield is another check, rather than a finding about the corpus, signals the
> machinery becoming its own subject. Three iterations converging on a criterion is convergence;
> a fourth refining the third is drift.**

The distinguishing question is not how good the check has become but **what it last produced**: a
finding about the mathematics or the record is yield; a better version of the checking apparatus
is not.

**Applied immediately, as its first use.** This stretch ran name-count → content-words-anywhere →
findability-from-the-mention. The third is a criterion, not an approximation. **THIS STRETCH'S
AUDIT IS DECLARED CLOSED — and the closing condition is a fact about the corpus rather than about
the instrument: the resolvability standard is met across the Correspondence table.** Every landed
row's named premises are now stated in the mirror. That was the object of the work, and it is
done.

**The rule's own guard against misuse**, since a stopping rule can stop the wrong thing: it does
not forbid building instruments — this era's instruments produced findings (the placement screen
caught two zero-power instruments; the compiled salt-check caught a vacuity risk; the provenance
sweep caught an unverified extrapolation on the day it was filed). **It forbids the specific
pattern of an instrument pass whose only product is a refinement of the previous instrument
pass**, and it is satisfied by any pass that returns to corpus material.

**Kin: the rule-hygiene clause** (a rule unfired for ten sittings is reviewed). **That one guards
against machinery outliving its use; this one guards against machinery outrunning it.**
