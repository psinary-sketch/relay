## Finding 1 — corrections are inside body sentences (the rule's main violation)

**Where.** §3's measured-anatomy block now carries two in-text withdrawal passages, both added
at v0.12 in good faith:

> *The figures first reported here — Z₉ = 1.62, Q₉ = 0.78, "10²–10³× the floor" — were taken
> against a merely density-matched control and were inflated by its counting-convention offset
> by 8–32×; they are withdrawn and replaced by the corrected values above…*

> *The first reported share was 97% against the density-matched control; against the
> position-matched control the magnitudes fall by 45–68× and the share settles at 92%…*

**Why it violates.** These are corrections doing their work inside the body's voice. A reader
meeting them mid-sentence reads the corrected figure as *hedged* rather than as *recorded* —
which is the opposite of the effect intended, since the whole point of stating them was to
keep the provenance visible.

**The drafted move.** Add a **PROVENANCE** block after §3 (before "Read together, the halves
interlock"), holding: the two withdrawal passages verbatim, the c-sequence VOID notice, and
the control-matching law's one-line cause. The body then states only the corrected figures —
Z₉ = 0.0507, Q₉ = 0.0951, the 92% share — each carrying a pointer to the provenance block.
**Nothing is deleted; the same sentences move to where their voice belongs.**

(*derived from pair correlation ⇒ consistency, never evidence; the channel falsifies and
cannot confirm*) **and** the placement of ζ among the three anchors — the second reads as a
result while the first says it cannot be one.

**The drafted move.** Keep the anchors table and ζ's measured value in the body (they are
measurements, and the body is their place). Move the *interpretive* pair — "this is
consistency, not evidence" against "this is where ζ sits" — into the Forward's open items,
as the undecided pair it is: **what would decide it is an independent placement-sensitive
statistic, which the arc has now shown the density register cannot supply.**
