## §1 — D-2a's ROW UPGRADE: THE DERIVATION LANDED AS SCOPED

**The registered expectation is CONFIRMED. The premise count moved from two to one.**

A new module, `FieldLayer.lean`, carries the layer: a field `K`, an element `s` with `s ^ 2 = q`,
and `s ≠ 0`. Nothing else — no ordering, no Galois apparatus, no root counting. The scoping pass
priced the full ℚ(√q) build and found two costs (an ordered structure by hand; Sturm chains
absent from Mathlib entirely); **this module takes only the part D-2a's link needs**, and takes
it as a hypothesis on an arbitrary field rather than by constructing the extension.

**How the stipulation converted.** The old hypothesis was a bare equation between two numbers —
`c g = ptTop q g p` — with no independent characterization of `c` anywhere in the statement. It
is replaced by the certificate's **defining relation**, written without inverses by multiplying
through by `x^g`:

```
IsExpansion g c pt :  ∑_{j ≤ g} C (c j) * (X^(g+j) + X^(g-j))  =  ∑_{i ≤ 2g} C (pt i) * X^i
```

and the identification is then **derived** — `FieldLayer.top_coeff_of_expansion` reads the
degree-`2g` coefficient off both sides. On the left only `j = g` contributes: `X^(g+j)` hits `2g`
exactly at `j = g`, and `X^(g-j)` cannot reach `2g` at all once `g ≥ 1`, since `g - j ≤ g < 2g`.

**Two further gains, neither registered, both filed as found.** The whole module generalized from
ℚ to an arbitrary field in the same pass — the old file was ℚ-specific for no reason the proofs
needed. And `lead_law` **lost its `q ≠ 0` hypothesis**, which now follows from `s ≠ 0`.

### The salt-check, compiled rather than argued

Replacing a hypothesis with a stronger-looking one is worth nothing if the stronger one is
unsatisfiable. Two checks, both compiled in `SaltCheck_LeadLaw.lean`:

- **`expansion_exists_of_palindromic`** — at `g = 1`, **every** palindromic normalized sequence
  admits an expansion, with the coefficients exhibited. Non-vacuity is generic, not a single
  point — and palindromy is exactly what the functional equation supplies, so the witness is of
  the shape the theorem's own hypotheses produce.
- **`hg_is_load_bearing`** — `1 ≤ g` is USED: at `g = 0` an expansion exists whose top
  coefficient is *not* the top normalized coefficient (the `X^0` term is `2 * c 0`, so the top
  coefficient is half the normalized one). **This is the OVER-HYPOTHESIZED check run forward** —
  the grade minted this morning says a hypothesis is load-bearing only when its deletion is shown
  to break something, and here the breakage is compiled.

### The shortfall, re-measured

**It stays ONE clause wide, and the clause's load halves.** The paper says *"since the proof uses
self-duality alone"*; the statement establishes *"from the functional equation self-duality
supplies, given Duursma's construction."* That clause previously carried **two** named premises;
it now carries **one**. The clause count is the honest measure and it did not move — what moved is
what sits inside the clause, and that is worth recording as its own quantity rather than being
rounded into the count.

## §2 — D-2c: THE IDENTITY INSTANCE, COMPILED

Module `TwoSidesIdentity.lean`. The registered statement of §0 compiled without alteration.

**The spectral side carries the content.** `heine_three` is a polynomial identity in **six free
variables** — a theorem about every three-atom rational measure, not an arithmetic check at one
point:

```
D3 = w1*w2*w3 * ((x1-x2)*(x1-x3)*(x2-x3))^2
```

with `D3` the determinant of moments, expanded along the first row. Depths 1 and 2 compile in the
same form.

**The coefficient side is definitional at this scope, and the salt-check found it sharper than
registered.** `ladder_three` — `b0^3 * b1^2 * b2 = D3` for the Hankel-ratio coefficients — was
**closed by `field_simp` alone**, with the `ring` call after it rejected as having no goals left.
That is the honest reading: at this scope the coefficient side is a restatement of the definition
of `b_j`, not a second route to the number. **All of D-2c's content is on the spectral side.**
The registered expectation anticipated a cited dependency here; what the compilation showed is
stronger and less flattering — the dependency is not merely cited, the compiled ladder carries
no content at all beyond the definition. Filed as the registration directed.

**The three-route agreement at the explicit instance** (nodes 0, 1, 2; weights 1, 2, 3): the
determinant route, the Vandermonde route and the ladder route each give `24`, compiled
separately. `two_sides_balance` records the agreement **and** that the common value is non-zero,
so the agreement is not two vanishing sides meeting.

**Salt-check.** `heine_three_degenerate`: when two atoms collide the Hankel matrix drops rank and
`D3 = 0` — so the identity is capable of both values, and `D3 ≠ 0` at the instance is a fact
about that measure rather than a feature of the expression. `vandermonde_square_is_load_bearing`:
with the square dropped the identity fails at the instance, so the squaring is not a harmless
normalization. Both compiled.

## §5 — THE KERNEL WORK-ORDER: THE AUTHOR'S CALL, PRESENTED AND NOT TAKEN

`ECondition.type_I_has_ostrowski` carries an inert `[Fintype Domain]`. Two one-line repairs:

| option | what it does | cost |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **DOCSTRING** — state the inertness, leave the statement | says what the theorem *means*: the finite domain is the intended application, and the record now says the hypothesis is not used | none — no statement changes, no call site moves, downstream type-checking unchanged |
| **GENERALIZE** — drop `Fintype` | says what the theorem *proves*: valid on any domain, and its axiom profile empties | changes which call sites match; every instantiation that passes a `Fintype` instance must drop it |

**RECOMMENDED: the docstring.** Three reasons, in order of weight. **(1)** The theorem's role in
the corpus is as the exclusion syllogism *for finite catalogues* — the intended reading is the
one the era actually uses, and preserving the authoring intent keeps the statement legible to a
reader who meets it beside the seven-class catalogue. **(2)** Generalizing changes the statement's
type, and every downstream instantiation would have to be re-checked; that is a real cost for a
theorem whose content is unchanged either way. **(3)** The audit's value was never the
generalization — it was the *record* that the clause is inert, and the docstring carries that
record exactly where a reader of the theorem will meet it.

**The case against the recommendation, stated so it is not buried:** the generalization is the
mathematically honest form, and an empty axiom profile is a real gain for a terminal the corpus
cites often. If the programme's preference is for kernels that say what they prove, that reading
wins and the recommendation should be overturned.
