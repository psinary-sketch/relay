## THE CORRECTION FLAG — one candidate, and it has deadline character

> **PROV-2 × the zero-power class.** PROV-2's recorded core is *"computing conservation strength
> κ(P, I) from specification (no distributional assumptions); classifying interfaces DARK / GRAY…"*.
> **The era established that a DARK-type verdict can be an artefact of the pipeline's own
> construction** — the Li-Toeplitz ζ arm returned a PSD verdict that was structurally guaranteed
> before any data was read, and two on-line controls (random ordinates; a single repeated
> ordinate) returned the same verdict. **If a κ-based classification can be produced by a pipeline
> whose inputs assert the property under test, then the method as recorded needs I-7's stage-2
> screen to exclude a class of false DARK verdicts.**

**Whether this is a CORRECTION of the claim or an IMPROVEMENT to it cannot be decided from what I
hold.** The worksheet gives PROV-2's core in **one summary line**; **the actual claim text is not
in my record.** **This is flagged, not asserted** — and it is flagged precisely because corrections
carry a deadline (April 2027) and a wrongly-dismissed one is expensive. **The next act on this row
is to read PROV-2's claims, not to reason further from the summary.**
