### **AND IT IS NOT A RESOLUTION ARTIFACT.** The deficit is *identical* at `n_rho` = 400, 800,
1600 and 3200, because the left endpoint is fixed at `exp(1e-4)` independently of resolution.

*other* end of this same grid — `np.interp` clamping silently and returning a number —
