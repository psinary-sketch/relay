## §1 — THE NAVIGATOR-COINAGE CHECK, FILED (loom, standing)

**The check.** A compression phrase coined in conversation is not a ledger term until it is
minted. **Before any cross-link, the phrase is probed against the ledgers; if absent, the choice
is stated explicitly — mint it against its existing home, or drop it and cite the home directly.**
What is refused is the third option, which is the one that actually happens: writing the phrase in
as though it were already an anchor.

**The reason, recorded with it.** Coinages carry real content — that is what makes them worth
coining — and a good one reads exactly like an anchor. **A phrase that compresses a true reading
is indistinguishable, inside a citing sentence, from a phrase that names a filed result.** So an
unprobed coinage propagates: cross-linked, then cited, then relied on, while the ledger it was
meant to point at never receives it.

**Kin, and the family is now three.** The **description rule** (text true when written, never
re-derived) · the **statement-versus-claim register** (the distance between what is established
and what a sentence claims) · and this. **All three are gaps between a sentence's authority and
its warrant, and all three close by the same move: probe, then state the status.**

### The check paid twice on its first application, and the second payment was against me

The three phrases were probed against the four living ledgers last pass: **count 0 for all
three**, and they were filed as unminted vocabulary rather than written in. **This pass probed
the RELAY record as well, and that found the homes — and corrected my own scoping.**

> **`Center-equals-weight` exists verbatim**, in bold, at `reports/2026-08-01-w-half-consult.md:32`
> — *"Center-equals-weight: s = 1/2 as the fixed point of the FE ↔ the weight-1/2 of the theta"*.

My count-0 finding was **accurate as scoped to the ledgers and incomplete as stated.** The phrase
is a **coined-and-recorded relay term that never reached a ledger** — which is a third status the
check had not named, and the dangerous one, because it is neither absent nor filed.

**So the standing form gains its middle term: PROBE THE LEDGERS, THEN THE RELAY, THEN STATE THE
STATUS — absent · relay-only · minted.**
