## §2 — W-LI, SCOPED AT ITS ENTRY FACE

**(a) The two clauses, at cite — BOTH HOLD.** Li's coefficients admit two equivalent forms:
the ZERO-SIDE form λₙ = Σ_ρ [1 − (1 − 1/ρ)ⁿ], in which **the real parts of the zeros appear
explicitly** (the summand depends on ρ = β + iγ, not on γ alone); and the ARITHMETIC form
λₙ = (1/(n−1)!)·dⁿ/dsⁿ[s^{n−1} log ξ(s)]|_{s=1}, **computable from ξ's Taylor data with no
placement input whatever** — Bombieri–Lagarias give the arithmetic expression and its
asymptotic, and the effective-computation literature computes λₙ numerically from ζ-data
without locating a single zero ([Li's criterion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li's_criterion);
[Bombieri–Lagarias, via the effective-computation paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0402168);
[the saddle-point account](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.01755)).

**(b) THE ENUMERATION IS REFUTED — first-class, and the null's grade tightens.** E-24's
enumeration (Weil functional · Hadamard factors · off-line ξ values) claimed every real-part
statistic either IS the wall or takes placement as input. **λₙ is a fourth member and does
neither**: the real parts appear in its definition, and it is computable without them. **The
currency null therefore stands with its grades sharpened rather than softened: the real-part
requirement remains DEFINITIONAL; the enumeration is now REFUTED rather than merely
"not shown exhaustive."** The honest consequence: the class of admissible candidates is
larger than the arc believed, and the question "is there a currency definable in advance" is
**re-opened at the enumeration level**, though not at the requirement level.

**(c) The template's three clauses against the λₙ-deviation candidate** — the candidate being
the deviation of λₙ from its RH-asymptotic (Bombieri–Lagarias's trend/oscillation split:
λₙ = λ̄ₙ + λ̃ₙ), at cite, no computation this face:

| clause | grade | reason |
|:--|:--|:--|
| normalized self-datum | **FITS** | λₙ is computable from ξ alone and normalizes naturally by its own trend term — the closest thing to p₀ the ζ side has produced |
| square-free-by-symmetry | **PARTIAL** | the functional equation is present in the construction (ξ's evenness is what pairs ρ with 1−ρ inside the summand), but no quantity is exhibited as the square of another; the toy's coefficient factorization has no counterpart here |
| pricing (attack / window) | **PARTIAL at best** | λₙ ≥ 0 prices DISTANCE-TO-TRUTH — how far the zero configuration is from satisfying the criterion — not the SELECTION WINDOW, i.e. not how wide the set of admissible configurations is. A criterion measures failure; a currency prices selection. The registered expectation stands. |

**The convergence, stated as the ferry asks.** E-1's screen classed **Li as a CRITERION
route, failing the carrier clause** — it supplies a test, not the positive object. E-9's
register lattice found **the weakest sufficient register to be R4 restricted to the
Bombieri–Lagarias diagonal family** — and that family **IS Li**. So the arc's cheapest
sufficient condition and its best currency candidate are the same object, and that object is
a criterion. **Two independent lines arrive at one place: the ζ side's most tractable handle
prices distance-to-truth, and the wanted poster asks for something that prices selection.**
That is the sharpest form the currency question has yet taken.

## §3 — THE SELF-REFERENCE READING (Tier N, cross-linked, not promoted)

The currency null has the Silence Principle's shape one altitude down. Where the Principle
says a mechanism's structural force vanishes at its identity element, the null says: **the
coordinate in which selection would be priced is unavailable in advance precisely where the
selection question is open.** The discriminating condition, proposed and not asserted:
**the coordinate exists in advance exactly when the catalogue is finite.** In the toy the
catalogue is finite at every n — the code's weight enumerator is a finite object, p₀ is one
of its entries, and the whole selection story is written in a datum available before any
question is answered. For ζ the catalogue is infinite and its enumeration is the answer
itself, so the analogous datum cannot be read off in advance.

Stated that way, the condition is the domain-Ostrowski condition **arriving from a fifth
direction** — after the exclusion, barrier, supplier and determinacy vocabularies, the
currency vocabulary reaches the same finiteness/completeness boundary by its own road.
READING grade; cross-linked to the Silence Principle, the domain-Ostrowski row, and the
five-vocabulary Forward; not promoted, and no keystone cargo added on its account.
