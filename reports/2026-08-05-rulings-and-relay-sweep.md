## §2 — THE RELAY-ONLY SWEEP

The coinage check turned on the corpus as it stands rather than on phrases arriving in a ferry.
**A reader of the reviewer mirror sees 21 files; a term whose only elaboration lives in a relay
report is, to that reader, an unresolvable anchor.**

**Operationalization, stated before the output because it decides what the numbers mean.** The
bolded phrase is the unit — bolding is how this corpus marks a term as a term.

| class | rule |
|:--|:--|
| **MINTED** | 2+ occurrences inside the mirror set — the mirror holds it |
| **RELAY-ONLY** | exactly 1 mirror occurrence **and** 1+ in relay — the home is outside |
| **MIRROR-HAPAX** | 1 mirror occurrence, no relay home — coined in place, used once |

**Result — 21 mirror files, 107 relay reports, 2600 distinct bolded terms:**

| class | count |
|:--|--:|
| MINTED | **731** |
| **RELAY-ONLY** | **148** |
| MIRROR-HAPAX | 1721 |

**THE PRECISION IS LOW AND IS REPORTED BEFORE THE LIST IS READ.** Occurrence count is a proxy for
"held", so a term defined once and well reads as relay-only. **The shape discriminant is exactly
that false positive** — flagged at relay×6, and defined completely at its single keystone
occurrence with its anchors table following it. Bolding is likewise a proxy for term-hood, and the
raw list carries fragments ("for rh", "same shape", "summary table") that are not terms at all.

**Hand-read shortlist — terms a mirror reader genuinely cannot resolve:**

| term | relay hits | why it is genuinely relay-only |
|:--|--:|:--|
| **the Jacobi link** | 5 | D-2c's Correspondence row *names* it as its cited premise; what it says lives only in relay |
| **two-witness barrier** | 3 | used once, elaborated outside |
| **γ − 1 − log m** | 2 | the superposition constant — and itself DERIVED-BUT-UNVALIDATED, so the mirror reader meets an unresolvable name attached to an unvalidated quantity |
| **m_h = vv†** | 2 | a construction named, not given |
| **de Branges spaces** | 2 | a whole apparatus behind one mention |
| **non-discriminating by design** | 2 | a verdict-shaped phrase whose criterion is outside |

**NOT REPAIRED THIS PASS. No defect is claimed against any of the 148** — a term may be perfectly
well defined at its single mirror occurrence, in which case the flag is the proxy's false
positive. The list exists so the wave-edit inherits it with its classes and its caveats attached.

**The one that most earns attention is `the Jacobi link`**, because it is the *stipulated premise*
of a landed Correspondence row: a reviewer reading the mirror meets a row that rests on a named
