# rowgen — the Correspondence row generator/differ, built + tested — 2026-07-29

Built `tools/rowgen` in `D:\relay` (public; emits from kernels, never papers). Two modes: **generate**
(per-terminal record: verbatim `#check`, verbatim `#print axioms`, docstring first paragraph, proof body
first line, and a **DEFINITION-ENCODED** flag) and **diff** (a paper's Correspondence table vs the records —
flagging status-vs-docstring contradictions, rounded profiles, stale pins, missing terminals, and
definition-encoded-graded-DERIVES). Files: `tools/rowgen/{rowgen.py, README.md, terminals.json, records.json}`.
