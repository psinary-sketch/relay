## Verdict, at grade

**The expected pattern is confirmed — and its honest reading is that it is a DUAL, not a new invariant.** Kind A problems lack a **geometry/positivity carrier**; Kind C lacks a **derivation**; Kind B lacks a **measure/method/bridge** — precisely the inverse of what each kind's difficulty *is about*. So the "absence-type" is the difficulty-kind read from the missing-structure side; the correlation is near-exact **because it is near-tautological** — the two co-vary by construction. It **confirms the typology from a new angle, but does not extend it** with an independent invariant.

**The one genuinely informative sub-finding:** within Kind A, the absences split cleanly along the **chiasmus** — the **arithmetic pole** (RH, Hodge, BSD) lacks a *geometry* carrier (surface, cycle, bridge), the **analytic pole** (Yang–Mills, Navier–Stokes) lacks a *positivity* carrier (measure, coercivity). The absence-type **recovers the chiasmus sub-poles independently**, which is a modest cross-check that the arithmetic/analytic split is real, not an artifact of how the pairing problems were first described.
