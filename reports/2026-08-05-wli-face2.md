## §1 — THE GEOMETRY, AND THE NOVELTY CELL

**(a) The Cayley map, verified algebraically here.** With z = 1 − 1/s = (s−1)/s and
s = 1/2 + it,

  |z| = |s − 1| / |s| = |−1/2 + it| / |1/2 + it| = √(1/4 + t²) / √(1/4 + t²) = **1**,

exactly, for every real t. **The critical line maps onto the unit circle, exactly**; and
Li's coefficients are the regularized power sums of the images: λₙ = Σ_ρ [1 − (1 − 1/ρ)ⁿ] =
Σ_ρ [1 − z_ρⁿ]. RH ⟺ every |z_ρ| = 1 ⟺ the z_ρ lie on the circle. One further identity, used
in §3: the functional equation ρ ↦ 1 − ρ becomes **z ↦ 1/z** on the disc (a one-line check:
1 − 1/(1−ρ) = −ρ/(1−ρ) = 1/z), so on the circle it is conjugation, and the moments are real.

**(b) THE NOVELTY CELL — searched before any claim, and the answer is: nothing here is new.**
Every ingredient is at cite. Li's criterion (Li 1997; Bombieri–Lagarias 1999) — standard. The
**Cayley/disc formulation of RH is explicitly in the literature**: the conformal map
s = 1/(1−z) carries Re s > 1/2 onto |z| < 1, and RH is stated as the absence of zeros in the
disc ([Li's criterion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li's_criterion); the Li-type zero-free
half-plane work, [arXiv:math/0507368](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0507368)). The
**Herglotz–Toeplitz theorem** — a sequence is the moment sequence of a positive measure on
the circle iff its Toeplitz forms are positive semidefinite — is classical (Herglotz 1911),
and "positive definite sequences are exactly the Fourier–Stieltjes coefficients of
nonnegative measures" is textbook. Adjacent modern work exists on the Hilbert-space side
([Li coefficients as norms in a model space, arXiv:2301.05779](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.05779)).
**FOUND-AT-CITE for every ingredient; NOT-LOCATED as an explicitly named "Toeplitz
reformulation of Li's criterion" — and since the composition is one elementary step from
three standard facts, NO NOVELTY IS ASSERTED.** Everything downstream is graded as a
*use* of standard material, not a discovery.
