## §1 — THE INSTRUMENT WAS NOT IN THE REPO

The family run's generator is **not in the corpus**. It was reconstructed from the register's own
description — Bombieri–Lagarias `λₙ = Σ_ρ [1 − (1 − 1/ρ)ⁿ]` over a base of `M` on-line ordinates
(conjugate-closed, `B = 2M` terms — the **mean level**, which is *why* `B := 2M`) plus one off-line
quadruple `{½ ± δ ± iγ}`.

> ### **VERIFIED BEFORE ANY NEW POINT: 10/10 of the family's measured first-negative indices reproduced EXACTLY, and all ten `base_min` values to five decimals.**
> The base is **the first `M` actual `ζ` ordinates**. *A reconstruction that reproduces ten measured
> integers exactly is the same instrument. Nothing weaker was accepted, and the first three
> candidate bases (integers, half-integers, an asymptotic approximation) were rejected on this test.*

**Reaching four decades required 10,000 `ζ` ordinates**, beyond what per-index `zetazero` supplies
in reasonable time (1.9 s at index 20,000). Built by **Riemann–Siegel in vectorised float64** and
**verified against exact `zetazero` at 7 checkpoints spanning the range: max error `1.1e−4`, no
index shift.**

**Two defects were caught by that verification, not by inspection:**
1. the `C₀` remainder was evaluated at **`2(τ−K)−1` instead of the fractional part `τ−K`** — a
   systematic `~0.2` bias in `Z(t)`, which manufactured and destroyed zeros;
2. a **grid step of 0.05 missed close pairs**, producing a silent index shift of ~2 by `k = 5000`.

*The first verification attempt failed 6/6. That is what the checkpoints were for.*
