### WHAT THIS ACT IS ACTUALLY CLOSING, STATED PRECISELY.
The trilogy is not three careless moments. ### IT IS ONE STRUCTURAL HOLE SEEN
THREE TIMES: in every instance THE CHECK AND THE THING CHECKED WERE THE SAME
ACTOR, and the record had no mechanism that could tell a run from a claim.
 b146 -- a scanner EXCUSED a live use and reported CLEAN (per-line exception).
 b148 -- a commit message ASSERTED its own compliance in the act that breached it.
 b151 -- a report CLAIMED an audit verdict for a check THAT WAS NEVER RUN.
### THE FIRST TWO WERE PATCHED AT THEIR OWN SITES. THE THIRD CANNOT BE, because
the defect is not in any tool -- ### IT IS IN THE FACT THAT A REPORT'S AUDIT BLOCK
IS PROSE, AND PROSE IS FREE.

### THE FIX'S SHAPE, AND WHY IT IS THE RIGHT ONE: MOVE THE AUTHORSHIP.
An audit block is currently written BY THE ACTOR ABOUT THE TOOL. ### AFTER THIS
ACT IT IS WRITTEN BY THE TOOL AND ONLY EMBEDDED BY THE ACTOR.
### A HAND-TYPED VERDICT BECOMES A VIOLATION RATHER THAN A RISK, and the closing
sequence gains a member that can detect one -- ### BECAUSE AN EMBEDDED BLOCK WITH
NO MATCHING SIDECAR IS A FACT ABOUT FILES, NOT A JUDGEMENT ABOUT INTENT.

  commit_selfcheck.py. Each writes its own audit block to a sidecar in data/ with
  ### the scanned counts, the verdict, a RUN TIMESTAMP, and a SELF-HASH.
  ### AND THE MEMBER THAT MAKES IT STRUCTURAL RATHER THAN ASPIRATIONAL: a verifier

  ### THE HAZARD REGISTERED: an emit mode can be run and its sidecar then EDITED,

  NOTHING CLOSES THE SECOND -- and the tool's header will say so rather than
  implying the convention is airtight. ### A CHECK WHOSE REACH IS NOT STATED WILL
  BE TRUSTED BEYOND IT.
