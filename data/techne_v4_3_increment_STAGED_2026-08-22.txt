ADDITION 1 -- THREE CONSTRUCTS FROM THE INSTRUMENT LINE (Xi.10-Xi.12)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Xi.10 Hard-Clause Inventory [NEW IN v4.3]
**Purpose:** Decompose any open identity or limit question into EXISTENCE /
RIGIDITY / VALUE, each clause owned separately.
**Procedure:** (1) State the question. (2) Split it into the three clauses.
(3) Assign each clause its own owner and grade. (4) At every quote site,
read the question under its inventory.
**The law it enforces:** compression of one clause never speaks for the
others -- a question is not "nearly closed" because one of its three clauses
is.
**Provenance:** I-13, the critical pass (campaign, 2026-08-21).

### Xi.11 Red-Team Pass [NEW IN v4.3]
**Purpose:** Adversarial read of a span of results at named targets.
**Procedure:** (1) Name the targets before reading. (2) Write the attacks AT
FULL STRENGTH first, with no eye to their answers. (3) Grade each from the
record only: REFUTED-BY-RECORD / STANDS-AS-RISK / UPHELD-FINDING. (4) Upheld
findings enter the ledgers first-class, in-act.
**The law it enforces:** an attack the record cannot refute is filed as a
standing risk, never smoothed; and the pass is run against one's own writing
as readily as against another's.
**Provenance:** I-14, the critical pass (campaign, 2026-08-21).

### Xi.12 Gauge Audit [NEW IN v4.3]
**Purpose:** Separate a result's invariant content from its presentational
freedom.
**Procedure:** (1) Identify the symbol automorphisms of the record. (2) Ask
what survives them. (3) Separate THE BREAKING (invariant) from ITS DIRECTION
(possibly gauge). (4) If a question concerns the direction alone, asked
absolutely, consider whether it is a pseudo-question -- and dissolve it
rather than answer it.
**The law it enforces:** the invariant content of a result is what survives
the symbol automorphisms; an absolute question about a gauge quantity has no
answer to find.
**Provenance:** I-15, the class-bit dissolution (campaign, 2026-08-21); with
a second instance at the orientation-dependence read (2026-08-22), where the
same shape dissolved an absolute wing's role in an identity's path.

ADDITION 2 -- FOUR CONSTRUCTS FROM THE SEAM INSTRUMENTS (Xi.13-Xi.16)
Placement rationale: the ORGANIZATION table calls Xi "Operational

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Xi.13 Meaning Table [NEW IN v4.3]
**Purpose:** Fix what an outcome will MEAN before the outcome exists.
**Procedure:** For every deliverable x every branch (land / partial-with-
named-step / stall), pre-commit: which downstream items unblock or stay
blocked; which existing results re-grade and IN WHICH DIRECTION; which
caveats retire, travel, or gain scope. Every cell filled before any
analysis; the act then READS its verdicts through the table and quotes the
relevant cells.
**The law it enforces:** no outcome can be argued into a different
significance after it lands.
**REFINEMENT CARRIED FROM ITS OWN FAILURE (2026-08-22): the cells must be
indexed by AXIS as well as by branch.** A verdict can be right about the
branch and wrong about the axis -- the founding case recorded an EXISTENCE
claim ("no functional is fixed") where the live axis was GRADE -- and a table
whose cells name their axis would not have accepted the sentence.
**Provenance:** the sense opening (campaign, 2026-08-22); the refinement from
the apportionment act's correction (2026-08-22). Kinship noted: the T7
pipeline's preregistration discipline -- one species, "pre-commitment of
interpretation", with two independent instances.

### Xi.14 Separability Law [NEW IN v4.3]
**Purpose:** Keep a multi-client debt from being reported as one thing.
**Procedure:** (1) Enumerate the clients. (2) Give each its own chain, its
own branches, its own verdict. (3) Name shared machinery openly so it is not
re-derived -- but do not let the sharing merge the verdicts.
**The law it enforces:** SHARING A LEMMA IS NOT SHARING A VERDICT. A report
claiming the species without naming the client is a violation on its face.
**Provenance:** the sense opening (campaign, 2026-08-22).

### Xi.15 Two-Seat Dissent [NEW IN v4.3]
**Purpose:** Turn a computation into a measurement OF THE INSTRUMENT, not
only of the object.
**Procedure:** Where two seats (director and executor; author and analyst)
both hold priors, THE SECOND SEAT REGISTERS ITS DISSENT IN ADVANCE, on the
record, rather than deferring or silently complying. The computation then
adjudicates between two pre-registered expectations.
**The law it enforces:** a single seat that is right learns only that it was
right; two seats required to disagree beforehand make the disagreement
itself informative -- and a conflation invisible while both agreed becomes
visible the moment they must differ.
**Provenance:** the monotonicity act (campaign, 2026-08-22), with further
exercises at the link act, the lambda-scale act and the apportionment act;
the calibration pair (a miss and a confirmation of one law, one act apart)
recorded at FINDINGS.

### Xi.16 Grade-Indexed Status [NEW IN v4.3]
**Purpose:** Prevent two true status sentences from reading as a
contradiction.
**Procedure:** Every status line on a joint or an open carries ITS GRADE AXIS
-- bench / derivation / kernel -- wherever a single word would ambiguate.
**The law it enforces:** "closed" and "open" are not predicates of a joint;
they are predicates of a joint AT A GRADE.
**Founding instance:** a joint recorded CLOSED (measured per cell, gates
passing) and, three days later, listed as RECORDED OPEN (undischarged in the
analytic chain). Both sentences true; neither stale; and for four days they
read as a contradiction that did not exist. THE FIX WAS AN INDEX, NOT A FACT.
**Provenance:** the tenth seam close (campaign, 2026-08-22); it extends the
two-leg ruling's discipline (one content, per-grade scopes) from statements
to joint statuses.

independent decided consequence is filed.
