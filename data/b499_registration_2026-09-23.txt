###     WRITE.** ### A TITLE target is the whole field, and is FOUND ONCE when the field is present.
###     ### ### **THE ENTITY SITES ARE COUNTED FOR (N3):** HTML character references (`&name;`,
###     `&#n;`) in each returned description, each a place where the field`s bytes and the screen
###     text differ.
### (2) THE WRITES, IN THE ORDER GIVEN: `21539068`, then `21520474`, then `21539167`. ### For each:
###     POST `actions/edit` (expect 201); the new metadata is the BANKED BEFORE-STATE`s `metadata`

###     ### ### **THE RECOVERY, SEALED HERE:** ### if the edit POST is not 201, stop. ### If the PUT is

###     ### ### **THE FETCH-BACK:** GET `/api/records/:id` WITHOUT the token, banked whole. ### The
###     platform may serve the old state for a few seconds after a publish, so the fetch is repeated
###     at 10-second intervals, up to SIX tries, until the returned title is the intended one, and

###     it; ### (iv) the returned description`s text EQUALS the intended description`s text, where
###     "text" means tags removed, entities decoded and whitespace runs collapsed. ### **ALL FOUR, OR
###     MISMATCH**; a mismatch prints the diff and STOPS, and the other records are left untouched.
###     ### The raw bytes are ALSO compared and the result printed, but a raw difference alone is not
###     a mismatch: the rule compares what the record SAYS, as (R110)`s "sentence-for-sentence" asks.
