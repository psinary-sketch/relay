phase1.5/proofs/THE_UNCONDITIONAL_SURROUND.md:25
  This is a reduction, in the sense that Lagarias's positivity criterion, the Weil explicit-formula criterion, and Riemann's own reformulations are reductions: it carries the Riemann Hypothesis to a single equivalent statement and proves everything around that statement. It does not claim the Hypothesis. It claims something exact and smaller — that the entire structure surrounding the zeros of ξ is settled unconditionally, and that what remains is one premise, named, typed, and necessary.
