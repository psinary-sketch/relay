b647 -- COMPONENT 2, (R257)(2): THE HELPER READERS' BRIEF FOR EVERY KERNEL'S DOCSTRINGS, WRITTEN FROM b646'S 27 CORRECTIONS AND BANKED
BEFORE ANY ROW IS READ (2026-10-10)

### THE TASK. A docstring of a kernel (a `/-- -/` before a declaration or structure field, a `/-! -/` module header, or a file's head
### `/- -/` block) is a row of the licensed-statement table (relay tools/licensed_table.py): SOURCE (kernel@commit:file:line), STATED (the
### docstring), LICENSED (what the kernel at that commit holds), VERDICT (exactly one of MATCHES, UNDERSTATES, OVERREACHES, UNLICENSED),
### ACTION (none for MATCHES; RE-CUT: <the docstring sentence as the kernel licenses it> for UNDERSTATES or OVERREACHES; a work-order or
### RETIRE TO ERRATA for UNLICENSED). The verdict is first GENERATED by the rule D1-D6 (licensed_table.py `docstring_row`) against the
### declaration's statement (elaborated where the kernel has a bank -- SIDE-structural-error-correction -- and TEXTUAL elsewhere, read from
### the source's header) and its grade in the terminal table (relay data/terminal_table.json at relay HEAD). A helper reader reads the rows
### the rule flags, and the seeded samples of the rows it reads MATCHES, against the kernel's source at the commit, and writes one line per
### row. The seat then reads every non-MATCHES row whole against its cited lines.

### THE VERDICTS, as b645 fixed them. MATCHES: every claim the docstring makes is what the cited lines carry. UNDERSTATES: the docstring
### says less than the kernel holds (a theorem the kernel proves called open, not proved, a premise, or a sorry; a Prop the kernel concludes
### with no premise called unproved). OVERREACHES: the docstring says more than the kernel holds (a declaration graded INTERFACES called
### unconditional; a premise the premise table reads OPEN or CITED called proved; an equivalence claimed of a one-direction statement; a
### register of the one open clause stated as proved or certified). UNLICENSED: no line of any kernel or citation reaches the claim.

### THE 27 CORRECTIONS OF b646 (relay data/b646_table_<companion>.txt, the rows the seat corrected after its whole read), READ AS RULES:
### (1) A CLAIM STATED AS THE DOCUMENT'S OWN ARGUMENT IS GRADED, NOT ESTABLISHED, AND MATCHES WHEN IT CLAIMS NO STATUS BEYOND ARGUMENT

### THE TEXTUAL READER, A CAUTION. Where a row is TEXTUAL the LICENSED cell is the source's header text, not an elaborated type: read the
### declaration whole (its binders and its conclusion), and the definitions its conclusion names, before agreeing.

### THE OUTPUT, one line per row, TAB-separated: id <TAB> VERDICT <TAB> LICENSED (one sentence, the precise difference for a non-MATCHES)
### <TAB> CITED (kernel@commit:path:N; ...) <TAB> ACTION. For a sampled MATCHES row: id <TAB> AGREE or DISAGREE <TAB> why <TAB> CITED.
### Read-only everywhere: git show and git grep at the commit; no checkout, no build, no write but the one output file by the Write tool.
