### (3) THE ROOM: FREE FOR ONE AXIS, UNPRICEABLE FOR THE OTHER.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### ### **THE CUT'S THRESHOLD.** ### At every rung b344 printed the largest eigenvalue dropped and the
### smallest kept. ### The threshold may sit anywhere strictly inside the INTERSECTION of those intervals
### across the rungs without changing which eigenvalues are kept.
###     the widest largest-dropped over the rungs  : `2.144048e-07`
###     the narrowest smallest-kept over the rungs : `2.277535e-06`
### ### ### **THE RANK-PRESERVING BAND IS `(2.144048e-07, 2.277535e-06)`, A FACTOR OF `10.62` WIDE**, and
### the corpus's own `tau = 1.0e-06` sits inside it: free to fall by a factor of `4.66` or rise by a factor
### of `2.28` with the same eigenvalues kept at every rung, and therefore the same rank.
### ### ### **AND THAT IS A FACT ABOUT THE CUT AND NOT ABOUT THE RESIDUAL.** ### The registration fixed
### this distinction before the arithmetic, and it is the whole of what this act had to keep straight: the
### same subspace kept does not mean the same residual, because the residual depends on how the kept
### subspace is used and not only on which vectors are in it. ### **b344 PRINTED NO RESIDUAL AT A SECOND
### ### THRESHOLD, SO THE RESIDUAL'S RESPONSE TO THE THRESHOLD IS NOT PRICED BY THESE FIGURES.**
### ### **THE TAPER.** ### `ALPHA` takes the value `1.0` at every rung and `BETA` takes `1.0`, and nothing
### is printed beside them: no second value, no neighbourhood, no eigenvalue interval, no derivative.
### ### ### **SO THE PRINTED FIGURES PRICE NO ROOM FOR THE TAPER AT ALL, AND THIS ACT SAYS SO RATHER THAN
### ### ### INVENTING ONE.**
### ### **AND THE PRICING IS THEN PRICED, AS THE ORDER REQUIRES:** to price the taper's room one would need
### the residual at a second `ALPHA` and at a second `BETA` -- ### **two ladders, `178.70` seconds of wall
### at the reference frame** -- and ### **EVEN THAT WOULD GIVE A DIFFERENCE AND NOT A ROOM**, because the
### taper has no analogue of the eigenvalue interval that gives the threshold one. ### **THE TAPER'S ROOM
### ### MAY NOT BE PRICEABLE AT ANY COST IN THIS SHAPE, AND THAT IS SAID RATHER THAN A NUMBER INVENTED FOR
### ### IT.**

### (4) WHAT EACH MOVE WOULD CONFOUND, IN THE SEALED WORDS OF THE ACT THAT DECLINED IT.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### Both reasons were located through the sortie's shared normaliser at b344's own sealed registration,
### lines `72` and `78`.
### ### **THE CUT'S THRESHOLD WOULD CONFOUND THE RANK WITH THE FLOOR.** ### Moving it moves the stable cut,
### and b343 showed the rank constant across the grid axis, so a residual that moved with the threshold
### could not be told from a residual that moved with the rank. ### b319 records that the corpus's
### threshold sits ### **57 TIMES INSIDE THAT SEPARATION**, which is why it was chosen and why moving it is
### not free.
### ### **THE TAPER WOULD CONFOUND THE INSTRUMENT WITH THE OBJECT.** ### `ALPHA` and `BETA` are the
### source's own constants -- b316 records them as *"Definition 4.4's `a`, at the source's own `S(1,1)`"* --
### so a taper moved is no longer the source's object, and any change in the residual would be a change in
### ### WHAT WAS BEING MEASURED. ### **THAT IS A HARDER CONFOUND THAN THE THRESHOLD'S.**
### ### ### **SO THE TWO AXES ARE NOT SYMMETRIC, AND THE ASYMMETRY IS NOT IN THE COST.** ### The threshold
### is cheap to move and its move is interpretable only against a rank that would move with it. ### The
### taper is equally cheap to move and its move ### **CHANGES THE OBJECT**, which no amount of wall buys
### back.
