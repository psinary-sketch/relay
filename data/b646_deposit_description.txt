<p><strong>THE CLAIM</strong></p><p>A PLACE TO STAND is the name of the research programme whose papers, kernels and ledgers this record deposits; it makes its claim in one sentence, as its README states it: "RH reduced to a single located clause, reduction machine-verified." Here RH is the Riemann Hypothesis, as Mathlib states it (RiemannHypothesis): every zero s of the Riemann zeta function that is not a trivial zero −2(n+1) and is not s = 1 has real part 1/2. The located clause: the Riemann Hypothesis holds exactly when the explicit-formula quadratic form Q(k) = pole term − prime sum + archimedean term is non-negative for every admissible test window k, the theorem h2_sign_iff_rh deriving the equivalence with no premise, and the open clause being the sign statement h2_sign itself (in its attribution face covers_all, over the seven-class catalogue complete over its named classes), which the kernel states, proves equivalent to RH, and carries as its one open premise.
