### (2) COMPONENT 1 -- THE CONDITION, DERIVED. ### [SEAT, PRE-RUN, FROM QUOTED TEXT]
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### ### **(a) THE TRANSFORM OF THE SQUARE.** ### From (147) and the involution, by the substitution
### `u = 1/x`: `(f^7)~(s) = INT x^{-1} f(x^{-1}) x^{s-1} dx = INT f(u) u^{-s} du = f~(1 - s)`. ### The
### convolution on `R*_+` multiplies Mellin transforms, so for `f = g * g^7`:
### ### **`f~(s) = g~(s) g~(1 - s)`**, symmetric under `s -> 1 - s`; and for real `g`,
### `f~(conj s) = conj f~(s)`. ### In the corpus's variable (b321: `f(x) = x^{-1/2} w(log x)`, so
### `f~(rho) = INT w(v) e^{(rho - 1/2) v} dv` -- b326_closure's `ftilde`), write `c := rho - 1/2` and
### ### **`G(c) := INT w_g(v) e^{c v} dv`** for the seed; then `f~(rho) = G(c) G(-c)`.
### ### **(b) THE FOUR-TERM SUM.** ### An off-line zero `rho = beta + i gamma` comes with its three
### partners `conj rho`, `1 - rho`, `1 - conj rho` (the functional equation and real coefficients).
### With the symmetries of (a), `f~(1 - rho) = f~(rho)` and `f~(conj rho) = conj f~(rho)`, so
### ### **`S_4 := f~(rho) + f~(conj rho) + f~(1 - rho) + f~(1 - conj rho) = 4 Re[ G(c) G(-c) ]`**,
### `c = (beta - 1/2) + i gamma`. ### **THE FACTOR FOUR IS THE QUADRUPLE'S COUNT AND IS THE FIRST
### NORMALIZATION CHECKED:** ### b326's closure tool sums exactly these four terms (its loop over
### `(beta, gam), (beta, -gam), (1 - beta, gam), (1 - beta, -gam)`), and its banked four terms at
### every arc cell must equal `4 Re[G(c) G(-c)]` formed from the seed alone -- ### **BAR (B1):**
### agreement to `1e-9` relative at the thirteen arc cells, else the derivation is wrong and the act
### says so first.
### ### **(c) THE EVEN SEED.** ### `w_g` even in `v` is exactly `g = g^7` (b321's line: `f^#(x) =
### x^{-1/2} w(-log x)`), which gives `G(-c) = G(c)`. ### Then ### **`S_4 = 4 Re[ G(c)^2 ] = 4 |G(c)|^2
### cos(2 phi)`**, `phi := arg G(c)`. ### **NEGATIVE EXACTLY WHEN `cos 2 phi < 0`, I.E. WHEN
### `45 deg < |phi| < 135 deg` (mod 180 deg)** -- the navigator's assertion (F1), DERIVED, with the
### quadruple's factor four in front. ### **BAR (B2):** `|G(-c) - G(c)| <= 1e-12 |G(c)|` on every even
### seed of this act, measured.
### ### **(d) THE GENERAL REAL SEED.** ### `w = w_e + w_o` (even and odd parts in `v`); `G(-c) = G_e(c)
### - G_o(c)`; so ### **`S_4 = 4 Re[ G_e(c)^2 - G_o(c)^2 ]`**. ### **WHAT A SEED NEEDS, THEREFORE:**
### an even seed needs PHASE -- `|phi_e| > 45 deg`; an odd component contributes `-4 Re G_o^2`,
### negative exactly when its OWN phase is BELOW forty-five degrees, `|phi_o| < 45 deg`; the two add.
### ### **(e) WHERE AN EVEN SEED'S PHASE COMES FROM.** ### With `delta := beta - 1/2` and `w` even:
### ### `Re G = INT w(v) cosh(delta v) cos(gamma v) dv`, ### `Im G = INT w(v) sinh(delta v) sin(gamma v) dv`.
### ### On the line (`delta = 0`) the phase is zero. ### Off it, the imaginary part is generated
### entirely by `sinh(delta v)`, odd in `v` and of size `delta v` where `delta |v| << 1`: ### **A
### NARROW EVEN SEED HAS A SMALL PHASE**, `|Im G| <= sinh(delta L) INT|w|`, and a large phase needs
### EITHER width (`delta L` of order one) OR a shape whose `cosh`-weighted cosine transform at `gamma`
### is suppressed relative to its `sinh`-weighted sine transform, or both. ### **BAR (B3), THE CHECK
### AGAINST b326's OWN SEEDS:** the phase of `mean_zero_variant(a)` at `rho_1 = 0.9532604747946607 +
### 16.290215720390393 i` (the library's first off-line zero, quoted) must satisfy `|phi| < 45 deg` at
### all thirteen arc cells and the sign of `4 |G|^2 cos 2 phi` must be the sign of the banked
### four-term sum at each -- exactly as their positive sums require; a cell with `|phi| >= 45 deg`
### and a banked positive sum refutes the derivation.
### ### **(f) THE TWO CONSTRUCTIONS, DERIVED BEFORE THEY ARE BUILT.**
###   ### **THE EVEN SEED, SINE-AIMED:** ### `w_e(v) = env(v) sin(gamma_1 |v|)` with `env` an even
###     envelope on `[-L, L]` (the corpus's own bump, `carto_atlas.bump(a)`, `L = log a`), plus even
###     bump corrections solving the two moments as b317 solves them. ### Then `Re G = INT_0^L env
###     sin(2 gamma_1 v) cosh(delta v) dv` -- an oscillatory integral at twice the frequency, small
###     for a smooth envelope -- and `Im G = INT_0^L env (1 - cos 2 gamma_1 v) sinh(delta v) dv`,
###     of the size of `INT_0^L env sinh(delta v) dv`, positive. ### **SO THE PHASE IS SET NEAR
###     NINETY DEGREES BY THE SINE, AND THE WIDTH ENTERS THROUGH `sinh(delta L)` -- IT SETS THE
###     MAGNITUDE `4 |G|^2` OF THE QUADRUPLE'S TERM AGAINST THE ON-LINE ZERO SIDE**, whose terms
###     `G(i u)^2` at on-line ordinates `u` are `[INT_0^L env (sin((gamma_1 + u) v) + sin((gamma_1 -
###     u) v)) dv]^2`: a double lobe of width about `1/L` around `gamma_1` vanishing AT `gamma_1`. ###
###     Width therefore sharpens the discrimination on both counts, and the widths registered in
###     section (3) are chosen so that `delta L` runs from below one to above two.
###   ### **THE ODD SEED, COSINE-AIMED:** ### `w_o(v) = sgn(v) env_o(|v|) cos(gamma_1 v)`, `env_o(0) =
###     0`, plus one odd bump correction for the single pole condition an odd seed has. ### Then
###     `Re G_o = INT w_o sinh(delta v) cos(gamma_1 v) dv`, of the size of `INT_0^L env_o sinh(delta v)
###     dv`, and `Im G_o = INT w_o cosh(delta v) sin(gamma_1 v) dv`, oscillatory at twice the
###     frequency and small: ### **THE PHASE IS SET NEAR ZERO BY THE COSINE, WHICH IS WHAT AN ODD
###     COMPONENT NEEDS.**
### ### **THE PHASE THRESHOLD IS THE DERIVATION'S, NOT A FIT.** ### A seed that does not reach it is
### reported as such.
