4. **The growth-class dichotomy is VOROS'S THEOREM.** Voros (*Sharpenings of Li's criterion for the Riemann Hypothesis*, arXiv math/0506326): **RH true ⟺ λ_n grows as ½·n·log n (archimedean-determined); RH false ⟺ λ_n exhibits non-tempered oscillations with exponentially growing amplitude (finite-part determined).** *The "stiffness dichotomy" an earlier bench sitting proposed to introduce is this theorem.* It must be cited as Voros's, and any keystone statement of it must say so.
