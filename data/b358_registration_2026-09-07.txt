### (B) WHAT COUNTS AS A LOCATED SHAPE. ### FIXED HERE, BEFORE ANY SOURCE IS OPENED.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### ### **A SHAPE** ### is a statement in a real source, pinned by hash, that has ALL FOUR of:
###   ### **(1) A MAIN TERM** ### for the Li coefficient `lambda_n` as a function of `n`;
###   ### **(2) AN ERROR TERM OR A BOUND** ### controlling the deviation from it;
###   ### **(3) AN EXPLICIT OR RECOVERABLE INDEX** ### beyond which the main term dominates the error, so
###     that nonnegativity for all larger `n` would follow;
###   ### **(4) HYPOTHESES THAT CAN BE UNFOLDED AND NAMED** -- stated in the source, not inferred.
### ### **A STATEMENT MISSING ANY OF THE FOUR IS NOT A SHAPE**, and the act says which one it lacks
### rather than treating a suggestive asymptotic as a closure.
### ### ### **AND THE DISTINCTION THAT DECIDES THIS ACT, FIXED HERE BECAUSE THE READING WILL TURN ON IT:**
### ### ### **AN ASYMPTOTIC WITH AN ERROR TERM THAT HOLDS ONLY UNDER THE HYPOTHESIS BEING PROVED IS
### ### ### STILL A SHAPE UNDER (1)-(4). ### IT IS SIMPLY A CIRCULAR ONE**, and Component 3 -- not
### Component 1 -- is where that is said. ### **THE TWO MUST NOT BE MERGED**, because *"there is no
### asymptotic"* and *"the asymptotic assumes what is to be proved"* are different findings about the
### literature and the second is much the more interesting.
