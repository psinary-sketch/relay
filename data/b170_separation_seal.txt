 ### THE QUESTION: what does a statement have to be, in the objects' own terms, to take
 different truth values on two members?

 STEP 1 -- WHAT A MEMBER IS. By R2 the per-mode form is w_n = a_n - b_n - u_n*resid_N with one
  global residual share vector u; by R1 the void gate holds iff sum_n u_n = 1 (b154, decided in
  Core at row 69). ### A MEMBER IS A CHOICE OF u ON THAT SIMPLEX AND NOTHING ELSE: a_n, b_n, A
  and resid_N are the DATA and are member-independent, and by R3 the two mode-diagonal objects
  "carry NO apportionment freedom -- their split is GIVEN by their owners, not chosen".

 STEP 2 -- EVERY OWNER-EXPRESSIBLE QUANTITY IS ONE LINEAR FUNCTIONAL. Any quantity the
  requirements can speak about is built from the w_n by a weighting; for a coefficient vector c,
    ### sum_n c_n w_n = [ sum_n c_n a_n - sum_n c_n b_n ] - ( sum_n c_n u_n ) * resid_N.
  The bracket is member-independent. ### SO THE ENTIRE MEMBER-DEPENDENCE OF ANY SUCH QUANTITY
  IS THE SINGLE NUMBER sum_n c_n u_n, AND c IS THE WHOLE OF WHAT THE VOCABULARY CONTRIBUTES.
  [b166 shows this survives the whole downstream chain -- integration against the window
  autocorrelation, the rescaling, the PhiK pairing, the coarse/fine split -- because every one
  of those operations is LINEAR. Nothing further downstream can create a dependence the
  functional does not already have.]

 STEP 3 -- THE SEPARATION CRITERION, IN ONE LINE.
  ### A STATEMENT SEPARATES TWO MEMBERS u, u' IF AND ONLY IF sum_n c_n u_n =/= sum_n c_n u'_n
  ### FOR SOME COEFFICIENT VECTOR c THE VOCABULARY CAN EXPRESS.
  Two immediate consequences, both decided in Core:
   (a) ### c CONSTANT ==> CANNOT SEE THE MEMBER. If c_n = k for all n then sum_n c_n u_n = k * sum_n u_n = k by
     R1's gate -- ### THE SAME FOR EVERY MEMBER. So EVERY sector-summed statement, and every
     statement about totals, separates NOTHING. [This is the inherited half: b154 via b158.]
   (b) ### c NON-CONSTANT ==> SEPARATES SOMETHING. Any c that weights modes unequally
     distinguishes some pair on the simplex.
  ### THEREFORE THE CLASS OF SEPARATING STATEMENTS IS EXACTLY THE CLASS OF NON-CONSTANT
  ### WEIGHTINGS THE VOCABULARY CAN EXPRESS -- and the question "is the class empty" becomes
  ### "does the requirements list contain a non-constant weighting, and what does it reach".
