### ### **(B1) THE LADDER'S COURSE -- COMPONENT 1.**
### The residual is `||(1 - S) u|| / ||u restricted to the free coordinates||`, b316's own quantity,

###   ### **(B1a) DIRECTION.** ### ### **FALLS** ### if the residual strictly decreases at all four
###   steps. ### ### **FLAT** ### if any step fails to decrease. ### A flat course refutes the
###   registered expectation outright.
###   ### **(B1b) RATE.** ### Fit `log(residual) = A + p * log(X)` across the five frames by least
###   squares and report `p` with the fit's own residual. ### Fit the SAME model to b321's
###   instrument-residual ladder at `a = 1.3` and call that exponent `q`.
###   ### ### **"COMPARABLE" IS GIVEN A NUMBER HERE BECAUSE OTHERWISE IT CANNOT BE REFUTED:** ###
###   ### **COMPARABLE MEANS `1/2 <= p/q <= 2`.** ### Outside that band the expectation's RATE half
###   is refuted even if its DIRECTION half holds, and both halves are reported separately.
###   ### **(B1c) THE READING.** ### If the course FALLS, the residual is the truncation's and the
###   derivation is not contradicted by it. ### If it is FLAT, the residual is the realization's and
###   Component 2 must explain a fixed fraction. ### **THE READING IS TAKEN FROM (B1a) AND FROM
###   ### NOTHING ELSE**, and it is stated before any definition is unfolded.

### ### **(B2) THE EDGE DIAGNOSTIC -- b316's OWN, IMPORTED AND NOT REWRITTEN.**
### `taper` replaces the hard cut at `X` with a raised cosine over the last eighth of the domain.
### b316 wrote it for exactly this question: ### *"IF THE VIOLATION OF CONDITION TWO IS THE STEP AT
### THE END OF THE DOMAIN, THIS MOVES IT; IF IT IS THE VECTOR, THIS DOES NOT."*
###   ### ### **THE EDGE** ### if the tapered residual is below HALF the untapered one at every frame.
###   ### ### **THE VECTOR** ### if it moves by less than a tenth at every frame.
###   ### ### **MIXED** ### otherwise, and then it is reported as mixed and not rounded to either.
### ### **THE TAPERED VECTOR IS NOT `u_inf` AND IS USED FOR NOTHING BUT THIS COMPARISON**, which is
### b316's own sentence and this act does not weaken it.

### ### **(B3) THE DECAY, AND THE SECOND ROUTE TO THE SAME EXPONENT.**
### b316's `asymptotics` measures whether `x u(x)` is bounded far out. ### If it is, `u ~ 1/x`, and
### the `L^2` norm of the tail beyond `X` goes like `X^{-1/2}`, so ### **THE RESIDUAL'S EXPONENT IS
### PREDICTED TO BE `p = -1/2` FROM THE DECAY ALONE, WITH NO REFERENCE TO THE LADDER.**
### ### **THAT IS A SECOND ROUTE AND IT SHARES NO CODE WITH THE FIRST:** ### one is a least-squares
### fit to five measured residuals, the other is a bound on `x u(x)` read off the vector.
### ### **THEY AGREE** ### if `|p_measured - p_predicted| <= 0.2 * |p_predicted|`.
### ### **AND IF THEY DISAGREE, THE ACT REPORTS BOTH AND NAMES THE DISAGREEMENT**, because two
### routes that are allowed to be reconciled after the fact are one route.
