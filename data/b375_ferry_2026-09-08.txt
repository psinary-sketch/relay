ACT b375 - THE KEYSTONE AND CLUSTER CENSUS. Number not claimed
by any unclosed ferry. This act is the orientation the author
ordered before any disposition runs; the four lists b373 and
b374 produced stay OPEN and are not closed here.

SCOPE: READS and CLASSIFICATION only. NO document repaired, NO
class conferred on a document that declares its own, NO
keystone rewritten, NO row edited, NO grade moved, NO deposit
touched. The product is a census; the dispositions are a later
act and this one does not pre-empt them.

THE RUBRIC, the author's, quoted in the registration and used
as the census's own test: SUPPORT documents gather a subject's
research at a point in time and may carry hedges, open
questions and working notes. KEYSTONES synthesize a cluster
AGAINST other available content - kernels, other keystones,
other clusters - and may not carry those. Both are in ongoing
internal integration; neither is peer-stage; release is by
subject cluster or by whole phase and is the author's alone.

COMPONENT 1 - THE POPULATION, read from content and never from
a path: every document in the papers repo classified KEYSTONE /
SUPPORT / LEDGER / OTHER, with the basis for each stated -
DECLARED (the document's own class line, quoted) or READ (from
content, with the sentence that decided it quoted). Report how
many declare their class and how many do not; a document whose
directory suggests one class and whose content reads as another
is reported at full prominence and NOT reclassified by this
act.

COMPONENT 2 - THE CLUSTERS: enumerate the subject clusters the
corpus actually has, from the documents themselves and from the
registry's own rows, not from directory names alone. For each
cluster: its support documents, its keystone or keystones, and
whether a keystone exists at all. A cluster with no keystone is
a finding and is reported as one; a keystone whose cluster
cannot be identified from its own text is reported UNASSIGNED
rather than assigned by resemblance.

COMPONENT 3 - THE INTEGRATION STATE, four columns kept apart
and never averaged into one status. For each keystone: (a)
WHAT IT SYNTHESIZES - the cluster and the support documents it
draws on, quoted from its own text; (b) WHAT IT CITES - the
kernels and terminals it names, and whether each is a named
terminal or a count; (c) WHAT IT WAITS ON - anything its own
text names as owed, open, pending or routed, quoted; (d) WHAT
BEARS ON IT AND IS NOT IN IT - material in the findings layer,
the faces ledger or the trails ledger that names this
keystone's cluster or objects and that the keystone does not
carry, located by the anchor tool and listed by anchor, not
summarized. Where a column cannot be filled from the document's
own text, it reads NOT DETERMINABLE FROM THE DOCUMENT and the
census says so rather than inferring.

COMPONENT 4 - THE RUBRIC APPLIED, measured not opined: for each
KEYSTONE, whether it carries what the rubric says a keystone
may not - hedged sentences, open questions stated as the
document's own, working notes inside synthesis text - using
b374's audit unmodified, with each instance quoted and located.
Report per document. Repair nothing. Then report the same for
SUPPORT documents as the control population, so the difference
between the layers is measured rather than assumed.

COMPONENT 5 - WHAT THE CENSUS DOES NOT KNOW, stated plainly:
the questions this act could not answer from the documents
themselves, each with what would answer it (a read, a ruling, a
kernel check), so the reconciliation that follows knows where
it must ask the author rather than the record.
