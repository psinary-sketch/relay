== 3. THE ANGLE FORM: EVERY POWER exp(n·log z), log z ONCE PER BALL
  The rule (b675's, data/b675_li_plan.txt :60-:61): a power zⁿ is exp(n·log z) and never repeated multiplication. log z is computed once per
  ball, about an exact point near its midpoint, so the ball of the logarithm sits away from the branch cut: log z = log z̃ + log(z/z̃),
  z̃ = the midpoint rounded to an exact value; n·log z then carries the radius n·rad(log z) and no more -- a factor two per j, where the
  repeated squaring of a complex box grows by about 2.58 per j at j = 24-41 (data/b671_tie_diagnostic.txt :48 to :65: 7.021e-14 to
  6.969e-07 over 17 steps, arithmetic) and by 5-100 per j past j ≈ 53 (OPEN_TRAILS :14396).
  The power sites of the b667 map, each replaced by the helper pw(z, n) = exp(n·log z):

    unit phase taken once; :433-:434, :442-:443, :458-:459, (ĝ/M)^(2^(j₀+1)) in the zero side, each exp(2^(j₀+1)·log(ĝ/M)); a screened
    zero's ratio, the ball [0, ub] (:456), raised as [0, exp(2^(j₀+1)·log ub)] (the power is increasing on [0, ub]; log of a ball holding 0

== 4. THE TIE RESIDUAL BY ALGEBRA, BEFORE ANY RUN (why the controls measure the instrument and nothing else)
  g0 is real and even, so ĝ0(z̄) = conj ĝ0(z) and ĝ0(−z) = ĝ0(z) (the map cites paperFT_conj_of_real and offScore_reflect at :244). The
  four tie points are z0, z̄0, −z̄0, −z0 (section 2), so us = [u, ū, ū, u]; F has real coefficients, so F(v̄) = conj F(v), and
  ph = [φ, φ̄, φ̄, φ]. Q has real a, b with Q(v) = r exactly, so Q(v̄) = r̄, and r² = Yv = −φ̄. Hence
      res at ρ and at 1 − ρ:  r²·φ + 1 = −φ̄φ + 1 = 0 ;   res at 1 − conj ρ and at conj ρ:  r̄²·φ̄ + 1 = −φφ̄ + 1 = 0 ,
  identically, at every j, for any input values. The ball residual is 0 plus the instrument's radius. So the b671 diagnostic's OBJECT
  (a ball wholly beneath the bar with |mid| + rad < 2^-20, read as "the separation certified small by the object") is a label the
  algebra fixes at zero separation for every control; a failed control can only be INSTRUMENT (its radius at or above the bar), and its
  |mid| figures (data/b671_tie_diagnostic.txt :48-:88) are rounding about an exact 0. The test cannot fire on the object, so this act adds
  a positive control that breaks the symmetry on purpose (section 7, P2), showing the control does fire when a separation is there.
  The kernel side: hQtie is supplied by coeffs_exist (PowerLimit.lean :686-:704, the map's own text at :356); the ball control checks the
