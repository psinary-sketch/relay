### (C) THE STATUSES A ROW MAY HAVE. ### EXACTLY THREE, FIXED HERE, AND NO OTHERS.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
###   ### **`SAYS THE WIDER SENTENCE`** -- the passage asserts that the corpus's objects ARE IN THE
###     SOURCE'S CLASS, or attributes that membership to the scan.
###   ### **`SAYS THE NARROWER SENTENCE`** -- the passage says what was RUN and what it decided, without
###     asserting membership or crediting the scan with it.
###   ### **`SILENT`** -- the passage describes an act or a check without saying what the check certifies.
### ### ### **AND ONE DISTINCTION IS FIXED HERE BECAUSE THE READING WILL TURN ON IT:** ### **THE WIDER
### ### ### SENTENCE IS NOT NECESSARILY FALSE, AND THE ACT MUST NOT SAY IT IS.**
### The corpus's `f` is built as `g conv g-sharp` with the source's support and vanishing conditions, and
### if the objects integrated were smooth they would be in the class. ### **WHAT b355 FOUND IS TWO
### ### SEPARATE THINGS, AND THIS ACT MUST NOT MERGE THEM:**
###   ### **(1) AN ATTRIBUTION FAULT** -- crediting the SCAN with establishing membership, when for an
###     object built as an autocorrelation the scan cannot fail and therefore tests the arithmetic.
###   ### **(2) A MEMBERSHIP QUESTION** -- whether the piecewise-linear object integrated is in a class
###     defined over `C_c^infty` at all, which `b355` graded `REFUTABLE` and ROUTED.
### ### **THIS ACT REPORTS BOTH AND CONFLATES NEITHER.** ### A row may carry the attribution fault and be
### perfectly right about membership, and the classification above is about ### **WHAT THE ROW SAYS THE
### ### CHECK CERTIFIED**, not about whether the objects are in the class.
