### DEFINITION 3.3.1: "A sequence f_alpha, alpha in I, is a C0-sequence, if and only if f_alpha in
### H_alpha for all alpha in I, and ### SUM_{alpha in I} | ||f_alpha|| - 1 | CONVERGES."

 DEFINITION 3.1.1 (the weaker notion, for contrast): "... f_alpha in H_alpha for all alpha in I,
  and PROD_{alpha in I} ||f_alpha|| converges."
 DEFINITION 3.3.2: two C0-sequences are equivalent iff SUM |(f_alpha, g_alpha) - 1| converges.
 DEFINITION 3.3.3: "The equivalence ~ decomposes the set of ### ALL C0-sequences into mutually
  disjoint equivalence-classes."
 DEFINITION 4.1.1: the incomplete direct product determined by "### ANY C0-sequence from (E)" --
  the (E)-adic incomplete direct product. LEMMA 4.1.1: those products are mutually orthogonal and
  determine the complete one.
