    ### **THE TWO PROJECTIONS:** ### *"The projection P ... becomes the multiplication by
    ### the characteristic function of the interval complement {x : |x| >= 1} and P-hat
    ### becomes F^-1 P F."*
    ### **THE SPECTRAL DECOMPOSITION (81):** ### *"P P-hat P = SUM lambda(n)^2
    ### |zeta_n><zeta_n| + R ... The operator R is the orthogonal projection on Sonin's
    ### space S(1,1)."*
    ### **THE CHARACTERIZATION:** ### *"Sonin's space S(1,1) is the eigenspace of
    ### P P-hat P for the eigenvalue 1."*
    ### ### **SO THE SPECTRUM IS `{lambda(n)^2}` TOGETHER WITH `1` ON THE SPACE**, and an
    ### eigenvalue is DIMENSIONLESS. ### A cut on it does not move with the grid.
