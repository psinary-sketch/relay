### ### **(P1) THE PROJECTION HAS AN EXPLICIT CLOSED FORM, AND EVERYTHING ELSE FOLLOWS FROM IT.**
###   `Son(p,n)`'s orthogonal complement is spanned by the ball coordinates together with the
###   residue-class indicators mod `p^n`, and the class of `0` IS the ball, so the two families are
###   orthogonal and the projector is
###     ### **`Pi[i][j] = 0` whenever `i` is on the ball or `j` is on the ball;**
###     ### **`Pi[i][j] = [i = j] - p^{-n} [i = j mod p^n]` for `i`, `j` both off the ball.**
###   ### **REFUTABLE BY ONE ENTRY DISAGREEING WITH THE PROJECTOR `b304` BUILDS BY GRAM-SCHMIDT.**
###   ### **AND ITS OWN FIRST CHECK, WHICH THIS SEAT RAN ON PAPER: ### `Tr(Pi)` FROM THIS FORM IS
###   ### `(p^{2n} - p^n)(1 - p^{-n}) = (p^n - 1)^2` -- THE DIMENSION LAW, ARRIVED AT FROM THE
###   ### PROJECTOR RATHER THAN FROM THE COUNT.**

### ### **(P2) THE COMPOSED MAP'S FRAMES, AND THE AMBIENT IT NEEDS.**
###   `theta(p^k)` carries `V(n,n)` to `V(n-k, n+k)`, so `theta(p^k) Pi` is ### **NOT AN
###   ENDOMORPHISM OF ANY SINGLE FRAME AND ITS TRACE IS NOT DEFINED UNTIL AN AMBIENT IS NAMED.**
###   The smallest frame containing both is `W = V(max(n, n-k), max(n, n+k))`, and the embedding
###   `V(r,s) -> V(r',s')` exists exactly when `r' >= r` AND `s' >= s`.
###   ### **REFUTABLE BY AN EMBEDDING THAT DOES NOT PRESERVE THE HAAR INNER PRODUCT EXACTLY, OR BY
###   ### `theta(p^k)` FAILING TO SCALE IT BY EXACTLY `p^{-k}`.**

### ### **(P3) THE TRACE REDUCES TO A SUM OVER THE MODEL'S OWN GRID.** ### In the ambient, with
###   `N = p^{2n}` and `P` the model's projector:
###     ### **`Tr(theta(p^k) Pi) = p^{-k} SUM_{t in Z/N} P[t][p^k t mod N]`  for `k > 0`,**
###     ### **`Tr(theta(p^{-k}) Pi) = SUM_{t in Z/N} P[t][p^k t mod N]`  for `k > 0`.**
###   ### **SO `Tr(theta(p^k) Pi) = p^{-k} Tr(theta(p^{-k}) Pi)`** -- a functional equation between
###   the two directions. ### **REFUTABLE BY THE AMBIENT-MATRIX ROUTE DISAGREEING WITH THE REDUCED
###   ### ROUTE AT ANY CELL OR POWER, OR BY THE TWO DIRECTIONS FAILING THE RELATION.**
###   ### **AND NOTE WHAT THE REDUCED SUM IS: ### IT IS THE SUM `b304` COULD ONLY WRITE FORMALLY**,
###   because at a non-unit `t` the model's `t^{-1}` does not exist. ### **THE UNTIED FRAME GIVES
###   THAT SUM A DEFINITE MEANING; IT DOES NOT MAKE THE MODEL'S VERSION OF IT CORRECT.**

### ### **(P4) THE ANSWER: ### ZERO AT EVERY NONZERO POWER, AT EVERY CELL, AND IN TWO REGIMES WITH
### ### TWO DIFFERENT MECHANISMS.**
###   ### **REGIME A -- `|k| >= n`: A SUPPORT ZERO.** ### `Son` vectors vanish on the ball, so their
###     support lies in `p <= |x| <= p^n`; the image's lies in `p^{1+k} <= |x| <= p^{n+k}`. ### For
###     `k >= n` these are ### DISJOINT ### and the composed operator is ### **IDENTICALLY ZERO, NOT
###     MERELY TRACELESS.**
###   ### **REGIME B -- `1 <= |k| <= n-1`: THE SUPPORTS DO MEET, AND THE TRACE IS STILL ZERO.** ###
###     By (P1) and (P3) the sum is
###       ### **`SUM_{t off ball} ( [(p^k - 1) t = 0 mod N] - p^{-n} [(p^k - 1) t = 0 mod p^n] )`,**
###     and ### **`p^k - 1` IS A UNIT** ### -- it is coprime to `p` -- so each congruence forces
###     `t = 0 mod N` and `t = 0 mod p^n` respectively, ### **AND BOTH OF THOSE ARE EXACTLY THE
###     BALL, WHICH THE SUM EXCLUDES.** ### Both counts are zero and the trace is zero.
###   ### ### **REFUTABLE BY ONE PRINTED NONZERO AT ANY CELL AND ANY POWER.**

### ### **(P5) WHICH KIND OF STATEMENT THIS IS.** ### The derivation in (P4) is general in `p`, `n`
###   and `k`; it uses ### **THE OBJECT'S OWN TWO CONDITIONS** ### (through the closed form of the
