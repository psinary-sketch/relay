  :615 ## 10.1 The Syllogism
  :617 The proof requires no novel principle. It requires the ability to check a finite list. The logical template is a syllogism — the same one Euclid used to prove that exactly five Platonic solids exist, Hurwitz used for the four division algebras, and the Classification of Finite Simple Groups used for sporadic groups:
  :619 1. All properties of X trace to specification S(X). (From Determination)
  :620 2. If property P holds at x, some mechanism in S(X) produces it. (From 1)
  :621 3. The mechanism catalogue C₁,...,Cₙ is exhaustive. (From Exhaustiveness, certified by a completeness theorem)
  :622 4. No Cⱼ produces P(x). (Checked individually)
  :623 5. Contradiction with (2). P(x) does not hold.
  :625 Every step is standard ZFC logic. What is new is the application to the zeros of the Riemann zeta function, using Ostrowski's theorem as the completeness theorem that certifies Step 3.
  :627 A reader who accepts the syllogism accepts the proof. A reader who wants to understand *why* the syllogism captures something deep about determined systems reads the Mechanism Theorem below.

  :943 ## 15.4 Exhaustiveness: Ostrowski's Classification
  :945 ξ(s) is completely determined by its specification n² → θ → ξ. It has no free parameters, no external inputs, no sources of properties beyond its specification. A zero of ξ is a property of ξ, and ξ is its specification. The exhaustiveness claim is not that every conceivable source has been checked — it is that the specification is the only source. The SIDE method applied to a determined system is not induction over cases; it is deduction from completeness.
  :947 **Theorem (Ostrowski, 1916).** Every nontrivial absolute value on ℚ is equivalent to either the standard absolute value |·| or a p-adic absolute value |·|_p for some prime p. This classification is complete — known for 110 years, not in dispute.
  :949 **Consequence (Poisson Exhaustion).** The transformation stage n² → θ → ξ(s) produces exactly three mechanism classes. Every derivation chain from the specification must factor through some place of ℚ — archimedean, p-adic, or global. By Ostrowski, there are no other places. No fourth mechanism class exists at the transformation stage. Combined with n₁ = 2, n₃ = 2, and n₄ = 0:
  :951 **(2, 3, 2, 0) = 7. The catalogue is exhaustive.**
  :953 An eighth mechanism class would require an eighth place of ℚ. Ostrowski proved that no such place exists.
