  INPUT             reads: the kernel's Lean environment at the tag, after its build on the hosted runner (no lean on this host) -- for each
                    declaration of the kernel's own names, its transitive constant closure: the traversal #print axioms makes (CollectAxioms over
                    each constant's type and value), widened to collect every constant reached and not only the axioms.
                    output: one runner step's artifact, a gz of one record per declaration (name, module, kind, the closure as names), banked as
                    data/bNNN_circuit_<kernel>_<tag>.json.gz beside a text hashes bank (data/bNNN_circuit_<kernel>_<tag>_hashes.txt: the gz's
                    sha256 and bytes, the runner run id, the tag's commit), the binary rooted through the text bank (data/b680_rules.txt row (1)).
  SERIES            reads: INPUT's closure of the kernel's main theorem, restricted to the kernel's own names.
                    output: the restricted closure in dependency order (each name after every name its value consumes), one name per line with
                    its module:line, in a text bank; the count of names and the longest chain's length beside.
  TERMINALS         reads: the main theorem's type (its Prop binders) and INPUT's closure of it.
                    output: the binders that name a premise -- HCount, HExcl, the clause's compiled face, or any other named Prop of the kernel's
                    own names -- each with its binder name and type as #print gives it; and every axiom in the closure beyond the standard three,
                    by name; a text bank, rows are terminals.
  SHORTS            reads: INPUT's closure of every declaration.
                    output: each declaration whose closure contains sorryAx, by name and module:line, with the first declaration on its path that
                    uses sorryAx directly; a text bank, rows are shorts; "SHORTS 0" printed where there are none, with a control (a planted sorry
                    in a scratch copy of the gz's records, read as one short) so a 0 is not a reader that cannot find its needle.
  WIDTH             reads: the types of the kernel's declarations and INPUT's closures over its own names.
                    output: the pairs of declarations with the same type (syntactic equality of the elaborated type, up to the names of bound
                    variables) whose closures over the kernel's own names meet in few or no nodes, the meet's size printed per pair; the count of
                    such pairs and their names; a text bank, rows are pairs. The bar "few" is the build act's to name and bank before the run.
  LOAD              reads: INPUT's closures of every declaration.
                    output: for each of the kernel's own names, the number of the kernel's declarations whose closure contains it; a text bank,
                    rows are names sorted by load, the main theorem's closure marked.
  CLAUSE-DOMINANCE  reads: INPUT for each of the faces kernels (the kernels the faces page names, at their tags) and the clause's compiled face
                    (the declaration the clause compiles to, named by the faces page's node list).
                    output: for each faces kernel, whether the clause's compiled face lies in the closure of its main theorem (IN or NOT IN, with
                    the path where IN); the count over the faces -- the reading that turns "a single located clause" from a sentence into a
                    computed property over the faces; a text bank, rows are kernels.
  READFIRST-CLOSURE reads: a ruling and ferry text (a banked ferry, data/bNNN_ferry.txt) in place of a theorem, and the relay and PLACE-papers
                    trees at HEAD -- no kernel object; the seventh reading, on the author's word at this act's faces tail prompt.
                    output: the set of banks and tools the text cites by path, and the banks those cite by path, transitively, each item with
                    the line that cites it (citing file :line, the cited path); the closure printed as a readfirst bank in the b688 form
                    (data/b688_readfirst.txt: rows are items, the path, the line or lines read, the unit of that bank's rows, what the act takes
                    from it -- the last two left for the seat's hand); the reading that lets the readfirst bank be computed rather than
                    hand-written.
  OUTPUT            reads: SERIES, TERMINALS, SHORTS and WIDTH's banks for one kernel at one tag.
                    output: a DOT bank (data/bNNN_circuit_<kernel>_<tag>.dot) rendered to PNG beside the hashes bank -- series drawn as a chain,
                    parallel pairs side by side, terminals as open ends, shorts marked; the PNG binary rooted through the text hashes bank.
