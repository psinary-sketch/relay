### (2c) WHY THIS SURVIVED -- DERIVED, NOT GUESSED.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ### The corpus's ONE cross-check against the source at this point is the one-sided
  ### derivative at the identity, which its header derives and the source's Lemma 5.4
  ### states; the two agree. ### **BUT THAT CHECK CANNOT SEE THE FACTOR, AND
  ### ### PROVABLY SO.**
  ###   Write the common integral as `F(rho)`, so that the corpus's function is
  ###   `rho^(-1/2) F(rho)` and the source's is `rho^(+1/2) F(rho)`.
  ###   The interval of integration is empty at the identity, so `F(1) = 0`.
  ###   The derivative of `rho^s F(rho)` at the identity is `s F(1) + F'(1)`,
  ###   and with `F(1) = 0` that is `F'(1)` ### **FOR EVERY `s` WHATEVER.**
  ### ### **SO A CROSS-CHECK TAKEN AT A ZERO OF THE FUNCTION CANNOT SEE A
  ### ### MULTIPLICATIVE FACTOR THAT IS FINITE AND NONZERO THERE.** ### That is the
  ### whole of why a corpus that checked itself against the source found agreement.
