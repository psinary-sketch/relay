(2) THE OBJECT. PowerLimit.lean (SIDE-explicit-formula v0.2
= 5c72cad) proves h2_sign_imp_rh_strip by the power-window
route: for a fixed base window g in classK and an off-line
zero ρ = 1/2 + δ + iγ (δ > 0), the zero side of the explicit
formula at g^{*n} becomes negative for all large n
(zeroSide_eventually_neg), so positivity at every classK
window excludes ρ. The detection-region theorem is the
finite form: positivity at the windows of support at most
L₀ excludes every off-line zero whose (γ, δ) lies in an
explicit region R(L₀). The region is explicit exactly when
the "eventually" of zeroSide_eventually_neg is replaced by
an explicit index j₀(γ, δ) — the one lemma of substance
b554 priced — since the n-th power of a base window of
support [−ℓ, ℓ] has support [−nℓ, nℓ], and the region is
{(γ, δ) : j₀(γ, δ) · ℓ ≤ L₀}.

  def h2_sign_upto (L₀ : ℝ) : Prop :=
    ∀ h ∈ classK, (∀ u, L₀ < |u| → h u = 0) → 0 ≤ weilTest h h

  theorem detection_region (L₀ : ℝ) (hL : h2_sign_upto L₀) :
    ∀ ρ, zeroOfZeta ρ → inStrip ρ →
      j₀ (γ ρ) (δ ρ) * ℓ ≤ L₀ → δ ρ = 0

zero of ζ; the salt-check applies: the conclusion must
unfold to Mathlib objects and no Prop of the module may
encode it.
