The three grades of §0 and the salt-check of §VIII are two runs of one object, now stated as it. **A cited claim is a tuple** ⟨*statement* · *certificate-type* · *pin/registration* · *content-named residue*⟩ — the statement it makes, the kind of certificate backing it, the pinned commit or registration where that certificate lives, and (if the certificate is short of the statement) the residue, named as content. The §VIII table below is the calculus's first worked instance: its columns *Terminal · Location · Grade · What the source says · Work-order* are exactly this tuple.
