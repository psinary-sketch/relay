ANSWER 1:
The claim, quoted from the README, is "RH reduced to a single located clause, reduction machine-verified." RH means the Riemann Hypothesis as Mathlib states it: every zero of the zeta function, other than the trivial zeros and s = 1, has real part 1/2. The "located clause" is h2_sign, which is Weil positivity on the class of test windows classK: for every admissible k, the pole term minus the prime sum plus the archimedean term of the explicit formula is non-negative. The theorem h2_sign_iff_rh proves, with no premise, that this clause is equivalent to RH. It is checked in Lean 4 at tag v0.26 (commit 82550e4) and uses only the three standard axioms. So the claim is that RH has been shown, by machine check, to be equivalent to one stated positivity condition. The document also lists other machine-checked theorems with grades. Some of these (for example rh_upto_platt and rh_iff_nb) rest on cited premises from the literature that are not compiled.

ANSWER 2:
The document says plainly that it does not claim "RH proved". h2_sign is open and is carried as an open premise, and "nothing in this record states that the Riemann Hypothesis holds." It does not claim that the cited premises (NymanBeurlingPremise, PlattTrudgianHeight) are compiled; they are cited from the literature. It does not claim that the premises with status OPEN are discharged, and it lists what would discharge each one. It also says that DOMAIN predicates are not assumptions, and that premises with status WITNESSED are witnessed only by a construction that nothing uses, so a compiled proof is still needed.

ANSWER 3:
The refuted premise is TrivialSummandPremise. Its status is REFUTED-BY-COMPUTATION. As stated, it requires a pole term to be zero, but that pole term is positive at an object the programme exhibits. So the premise is false, no witness of it can exist, and it is not counted as OPEN. It is replaced by TrivialSummandPremise', which carries the pole term in place of the zero. The theorem dedekind_rhs' is proved on this restated premise. The theorem not_trivialSummandPremise (graded DERIVES) appears to be the machine-checked refutation, but the document does not say so in as many words.
