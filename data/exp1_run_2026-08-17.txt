  IF E2(L) is at the one-prime residual scale at every L, with no trend in the
  number of live lags, THEN: the extra prime powers put no measurable weight on

  two-prime room unchanged; and the union reading is confirmed vacuous as a

  number of live lags is the experiment's finding.
