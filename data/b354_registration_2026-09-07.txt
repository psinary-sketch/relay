### (D) THE CRITERION. ### **b352's, IMPORTED AND NOT REPLACED. ### THE ORDER SAYS "NOT A NEW ONE".**
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### The three models and the one criterion are b352's, sealed there before any fit and quoted here:
###   ### `M1 = A X^(-p)` (`k = 2`); `M2 = A X^(-p) + c` (`k = 3`); `M3 = A X^(-p) + B X^(-p-1)` (`k = 3`).
###   ### *"ALL THREE MODELS ARE FITTED BY MINIMISING THE SUM OF SQUARED RESIDUALS OF `log R`."*
###   ### `AICc = n log(S/n) + 2k + 2k(k+1)/(n - k - 1)`, lower preferred, and a model with
###   ### `n - k - 1 <= 0` marked `NOT SCOREABLE ON THIS LADDER`.
###   ### **BAR 1:** two scoreable models are DISTINGUISHABLE when their `AICc` differ by more than `2.0`.
###   ### **BAR 2:** `M2`'s `c` counts as a POSITIVE FLOOR only when `c > 0` AND `c` exceeds the `M2` fit's
###   ### own scatter at the last rung AND `c > 5e-10`.
### ### ### **THE MODEL FUNCTIONS AND THE MINIMISER ARE IMPORTED FROM `tools/b352_fit.py`, NEVER COPIED.**
### **A CRITERION RETYPED IS A SECOND CRITERION**, and the order's whole point in clause (ii) is that this
### act reuses the first.
### ### **WHAT CHANGES, AND IT IS ONLY THIS: `n` GOES FROM `5` TO `6`.** ### At `n = 6` the `AICc` penalty
### for a third parameter is `2 + 24/(n-4) - 12/(n-3) = 2 + 12 - 4 = 10` instead of `20`. ### **THAT IS
### ### ARITHMETIC OF THE SEALED CRITERION AND NOT A CHANGE TO IT**, and it is written here before the
### frame lands so that a halved penalty cannot later read as a tuned one.

### (E) THE BARS ON THE FRAME ITSELF. ### EACH WITH THE FLOOR OF THE OBJECT IT TESTS.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### ### **BAR 3 -- THE IDENTITY CONTROL.** ### The frame's identity trace must equal its `dim` to `1e-9`
### absolute, which is the control `b320` applied at every one of its frames and this act applies
### unchanged.
###   ### **ITS FLOOR:** ### the trace is a sum of `dim` terms each of order one, `dim` near `32000`, so
###   ### accumulated rounding is of order `dim * eps ~ 7e-12`. ### **THE BAR SITS ABOVE THAT FLOOR BY
###   ### ABOUT TWO ORDERS AND IS THEREFORE STRICT RATHER THAN UNINFORMATIVE.**
### ### **BAR 4 -- THE REPRODUCTION.** ### Before the sixth rung is computed, the FIFTH is recomputed here
### and must reproduce `b320`'s banked `domain` value at every covered cell to `1e-9` relative.
###   ### **ITS FLOOR:** ### `b320` banked those values at full double precision in
###   ### `data/b320_rows.json`, so the floor is machine epsilon on a quantity of order `8`, about
###   ### `2e-15` relative. ### **THE BAR SITS ABOVE IT AND IS STRICT.**
###   ### ### **AND THIS IS THE ARM THAT LICENSES EVERYTHING ELSE.** ### If the instrument does not
###   ### reproduce the ladder the record already holds, ### **THE SIXTH RUNG IS NOT A SIXTH RUNG OF THAT
###   ### LADDER**, and no fit may be reported. ### `IF IT FAILS, THE ACT STOPS AND SAYS SO.`

### one frame checked against its own dimension and needs no other act; bar 4 compares this run's numbers
