== THE CLAIM ==
A PLACE TO STAND is the name of the research programme whose papers, kernels and ledgers this record deposits; it makes its claim in one sentence, as its README states it: "RH reduced to a single located clause, reduction machine-verified." Here RH is the Riemann Hypothesis, as Mathlib states it (RiemannHypothesis): every zero s of the Riemann zeta function that is not a trivial zero −2(n+1) and is not s = 1 has real part 1/2. The located clause: the Riemann Hypothesis holds exactly when the explicit-formula quadratic form Q(k) = pole term − prime sum + archimedean term is non-negative for every admissible test window k, the theorem h2_sign_iff_rh deriving the equivalence with no premise, and the open clause being the sign statement h2_sign itself (in its attribution face covers_all, over the seven-class catalogue complete over its named classes), which the kernel states, proves equivalent to RH, and carries as its one open premise. On exhaustiveness: the seven-class catalogue is complete over its named classes; that every zero is produced by a named class (covers_all) is the open clause in its attribution face, equivalent to h2_sign given the surround, and not a separate obligation -- in the words of THE_UNCONDITIONAL_SURROUND, section 6a: "The attribution face asks whether every zero is single-class-produced (covers_all); the geometric face asks whether the two jaws meet at every height. Both are equivalent to RH given the surround." h2_sign is Weil positivity on classK: for every test window k in classK, 0 ≤ P(k) − PR(k) + A(k), the pole term, the prime sum and the archimedean term of the explicit formula; open, and carried as the one open premise. h2_sign is the one open clause of the reduction: the dependency path from h2_sign to RiemannHypothesis passes through the seam rh_strip_imp_rh, a classical fact the kernel compiles (rh_strip_imp_rh_holds), and consumes no OPEN premise; the OPEN premises in the table belong to the faces, instances and bounds, none on that path [in plain words: every OPEN premise in the table is assumed only by theorems the reduction from h2_sign to RH does not use], and the table is the keystone census at v0.7.1, in this record's files. classK is the admissible test windows: functions k on the reals that are even, twice continuously differentiable and compactly supported, and of the form h ⋆ h~ with h twice continuously differentiable and compactly supported. The ceiling, in the README's words: supportable, "RH reduced to a single located clause, reduction machine-verified"; not supported, "RH proved" -- the corpus does not support that sentence, since h2_sign is open, and nothing in this record states that the Riemann Hypothesis holds.

== WHAT THE LOAD-BEARING THEOREMS ASSUME ==
A premise is a binder asserting a Prop about a fixed object the kernel names and does not derive: a structure of premises, an EF_lit_* hypothesis, a Fact instance, a Summable or HasSum of the kernel's own series; a HINGE is a premise of one of the five programme statuses (OPEN, CITED, DISCHARGED, WITNESSED, REFUTED-BY-COMPUTATION) whose consumers lie in more than one kernel, or in more than one chain within a kernel, the chains taken after the premise's own evidence is removed from the use-graph; a premise of status DOMAIN, a Mathlib predicate restricting a quantified variable, is printed with its chains and is not a hinge. A premise's own evidence is, in the glossary's words, every declaration that uses the premise directly and either sits in a SaltCheck module (a Salt witness), or concludes the premise negated (a refutation), or concludes the premise itself, alone or under existential binders, with no hypothesis naming it -- bare (a witness) or under other hypotheses (a sufficient condition or a discharge); the premise's projections and constructors are its own names and leave the use-graph with it. [In plain words: own evidence is any declaration that is about the premise itself -- showing it can hold, cannot hold, or holds under a condition -- rather than a use of it.] [In plain words: a hinge is a premise that separate lines of the kernels' reasoning lean on, counted once the declarations that only test, refute or instance the premise itself are set aside.] A name beginning W-ORD- is a work-order on OPEN_TRAILS: a named piece of work, priced and entered, which an act takes up on the author's word; OPEN_TRAILS is, in its own words, "the cross-session continuity ledger -- items needing explicit follow-through: open trails, work-orders (W-ORD-*), and research-arc entries with dated addenda". A salt check is a test file that only shows a premise is not vacuous, that is, that it can be satisfied, so that a theorem resting on it is not true merely because nothing satisfies it. == WHAT IS OPEN ==
h2_sign: Weil positivity on classK: for every test window k in classK, 0 ≤ P(k) − PR(k) + A(k), the pole term, the prime sum and the archimedean term of the explicit formula; open, and carried as the one open premise. The squeeze between the zero-free region pressing in from the line of real part one and the transversality at the critical line has one concrete target, in the words of THE_UNCONDITIONAL_SURROUND: the jaws overlap at every height iff there is a uniform lower bound on |ξ′(ρ)| — equivalently |ζ′(ρ)| — at the zeros, large enough that the transversality obstruction reaches the zero-free region. Its faces, the same text says, are two statements of one open node: "Both are equivalent to RH given the surround", as h2_sign is by h2_sign_iff_rh; the squeeze's target is that node seen geometrically, not a second open premise. Its attribution face is the exhaustiveness clause stated under THE CLAIM.
