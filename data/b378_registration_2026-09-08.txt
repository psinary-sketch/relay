### ### GATE WAS RUN AGAINST THE RIGHT THING.**

### ### **`tools/gate_hash.py`, NEW AND SHARED:** ### it appends the `sha256` of a gate's SUBJECT to
### that gate's own run record, and reads it back. ### **IT APPENDS AND NEVER REWRITES**, so the
### gate's own output survives exactly as the gate wrote it and the stamp is visibly the caller's
### addition. ### Re-stamping replaces only a previous stamp, so a re-run leaves no contradictory pair.
### ### **`tools/b378_lockgate.py`, NEW:** ### it reads every pre-lock gate as `b376`'s did, ### **AND
### ### FOR EVERY GATE WHOSE SUBJECT IS THIS FACE IT REQUIRES A STAMP EQUAL TO THE FACE'S OWN
### ### `sha256`.** ### A gate carrying no stamp is ### **REFUSED, NOT WAVED THROUGH** -- an absent
### claim is not a true one.
### ### **AND IT IS FIXTURED IN FOUR POLARITIES, NOT TWO:** ### a clean set must permit; a missing
### pass phrase must refuse; ### **A STALE SUBJECT DIGEST MUST REFUSE**; and a missing stamp must
### refuse -- and each must refuse ### **FOR THAT GATE** ### rather than merely refuse.
### ### ### **WHAT IT STILL DOES NOT PROVE, SAID PLAINLY RATHER THAN LEFT TO BE DISCOVERED:** ### the
### stamp is ### **THE CALLER'S CLAIM ABOUT WHAT IT FED THE GATE**, not the gate vouching for itself.
### Making the gate vouch for itself would mean editing the shared instruments, and ### **THIS ACT
### ### EDITS NO OWNER INSTRUMENT.** ### The claim is nonetheless strictly more than the pass phrase
