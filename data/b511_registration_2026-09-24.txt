### ### THE READINGS OF THE ORDER, DECLARED IN ADVANCE:
### **READING (1) -- THE WINDOW.** For width `a` and B-spline order `m`: three uniform order-`m` B-spline
### bumps `phi_L` (knots evenly over `[-L, L]`, integral 1, transform `sinc(u h / 2)^m`, `h = 2L/m`) at the
### ladder`s own three widths `L = log(a^e)`, `e` in `(1, 1/2, 1/4)`, combined as `g = phi_1 + c1 phi_2 +
### c2 phi_3` with `INT g = 0` and `INT g cosh(v/2) = 0` -- the ladder`s pole-annihilating construction,
### carried over. ### The test function is `k = g ⋆ g~`; `g` is real and even, so `g~ = g` and
### `k-hat = g-hat^2`, exact in closed form at real and complex arguments. ### `k(x)` for the prime sums
### is formed from `g` by Gauss-Legendre on the pieces between breakpoints, exact for the degree, at two
### orders.
### **READING (2) -- THE B-SPLINE FAMILY** is `m = 4`, the cubic B-splines (R120)(1) names.
### **READING (3) -- THE NOTCH FAMILY.** `g_notch = PROD_j (1 + gamma_j^-2 d^2/dv^2) g`, whose transform is
### `g-hat(u) PROD_j (1 - u^2 / gamma_j^2)`: it vanishes at `+-gamma_j`, still vanishes at `0` and at
### `+-i/2`, so the pole stays annihilated; `k = g_notch ⋆ g_notch` vanishes doubly there. ### `gamma_j`
### are the lowest `k` on-line ordinates OF THE OBJECT IT IS APPLIED TO -- xi`s from the atlas bank, Q0`s
### from the completed bank -- so xi and Q0 are measured on different notch windows at the same widths.
### ### **FOR `g_notch` TO BE C^2 THE BASE ORDER MUST BE `m = 2k + 4`** (a spline of order `m` is `C^(m-2)`,
### and `2k` derivatives are taken): `m = 6, 10, 14` for `k = 1, 3, 5`.
### **READING (4) -- MEMBERSHIP IN classK**, read off H2Sign.lean`s definition: `k` is even (`g` even); `C^2`
### (a convolution of two compactly supported `C^2` functions); compactly supported (on `[-2 log a, 2 log a]`);
### and `k = Zeta23.EF.weilTest h h` WITH `h = g` (resp. `g_notch`), itself `C^2` and compactly supported --
### the seat`s statement from the definition, not a compiled step.
### **READING (5) -- THE CHAIN** is b504`s `cell` with the transform replaced by the closed form and nothing
### else: `Z` (xi: `2 SUM k-hat(gamma)` over the 10,000 atlas ordinates; Q0: over its on-line ordinates plus
### its 17 off-line zeros from b506 with their three images), `A` on b504`s u-grids against b504`s banked
### kernels (the atlas kernel for xi, b326`s derived kernel for Q0), `PR` (xi: `2 log p n^(-1/2) k(log n)`;
### Q0: b325`s `LAMQ`), `P = k-hat(i/2) + k-hat(-i/2)`, `r = Z - (P - PR + A)`, `m = A - PR`.
### ### **(R114)`S GRID RULE HAS NO OBJECT HERE**: it chose the v-grid so that the transform of a
### piecewise-linear window did not alias; the transform is now exact in closed form, no v-grid enters,
### and the rule is read as satisfied vacuously and declared so. ### b504`s u-grids are kept.
### **READING (6) -- THE BOUND, FIXED HERE: `B = E_u + E_k + E_tail + E_round`.** `E_u` is b504`s u-grid
### term (the half and wide grids against the base); `E_k` the prime channel`s difference between two
### quadrature orders; `E_tail` the zero-side truncation above the bank`s top (xi 9877.78, Q0 150), priced
### by an analytic majorant of `|k-hat|` (with `|sin w| <= cosh(Im w)`, Q0`s off-line reach `|Im| <= 1/2`)
### against the zero density (xi `log(t / 2 pi) / 2 pi`, Q0 b506`s `log(t sqrt 23 / 2 pi) / pi`);
### `E_round` the float floor, `4 eps` times the summed absolute size of every term, plus the ordinates`
### precision (xi float-accurate, `2 eps` relative; Q0 `6.1e-11`, b506`s fixture) against `|k-hat|`s change.
### ### **THE FLOOR IS PART OF THE BOUND BY CONSTRUCTION**, so no cell is refused for being finer than the
### float carries -- b347`s species. ### A cell is VERIFIED iff `|r| <= B`. ### No factor is fitted after
### the numbers are seen.
### **READING (7) -- THE FIXTURE (Component 0):** at `a = 5`, one B-spline window (`m = 4`) and one notch
### window (`k = 5`, `m = 14`, xi`s ordinates): the closed form against `INT k(x) cos(u x) dx` by 24-point
### Gauss-Legendre on `k`s own breakpoints, `k(x)` formed from `g` by its own quadrature, at six `u`;
### **the bar: agree to 1e-10 in absolute value, against a floor near 1e-16 times the transform`s size --
### the same function by two routes that share no quadrature and no
### transform formula: they share only `g`.**
### **READING (8) -- "SURVIVE WITHIN ONE CELL":** an extremum of xi`s `m(a)` over the verified B-spline cells,
