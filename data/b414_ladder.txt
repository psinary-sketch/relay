THE AFFORDABILITY LADDER, MEASURED BASE BY BASE AT LEVEL n = 1.
work at a base = |units p 1| x |range (gridN p 1)| = (p^2 - p) x p^2, i.e. grows like p^4.

  p =  4   work        192   DECIDED, axiom-free
  p =  6   work      1,080   DECIDED, axiom-free
  p =  8   work      3,584   DECIDED, axiom-free
  p =  9   work      5,832   DECIDED, axiom-free   (needs maxRecDepth above the default)
  p = 10   work      9,000   DECIDED, axiom-free   (9 and 10 together in 8.5s)
  p = 12   work     19,008   NOT DECIDED -- ran 292s at maxRecDepth 2000000 and
                             maxHeartbeats 1000000 and did not land; killed at the budget.
  p = 14   work     35,672   NOT ATTEMPTED -- larger than a base already over budget.
  p = 15   work     47,250   NOT ATTEMPTED -- likewise.
  p = 16   work     61,440   NOT ATTEMPTED -- likewise.
  p = 26   work    439,400   NOT ATTEMPTED -- b413 decided it in Python in milliseconds.

THE CLIFF IS SHARP AND IT IS NOT PROPORTIONAL. From p = 10 to p = 12 the work rises
2.1x and the time rises by more than 28x. Kernel reduction is not linear in the work
it does, so the frontier cannot be predicted from the work count alone -- it has to be
measured, and it was.

AND THE DEFAULT RECURSION DEPTH IS A TRAP, NOT A LIMIT. At the default, a decide that
runs out does not fail cleanly: it yields a terminal carrying sorryAx while the build
prints an error. Measured in the scratch probe -- three of four terminals compiled to
sorryAx and the fourth was clean, and only the printed profile distinguished them.
