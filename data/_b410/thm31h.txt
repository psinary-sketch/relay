**Theorem 3.1-H (relativized form).** *Let `M` be a determined structure with specification `S`, let `I` be an interface with `κ(P, I) = 0` for target parameter `P`, and let `H` be a finite set of `ℒ`-sentences **every member of which factors through `I` for `P`** in the sense of Definition 2.5. Let `π` be a formal first-order proof in `ZFC ∪ S` of `H ⟹ (∀x ∈ M: P(x))`. If `π` factors through `I` for `P`, then `π` does not establish that statement; the strongest statement `π` can establish has the form `H ⟹` (`P` holds on a density-one subset of each `I`-class), and the individual element remains unreached.*
