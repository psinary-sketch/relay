The theorems assume zeros are simple. Under GRH for Dirichlet L-functions, all zeros of ζ are simple. The best unconditional result is that a positive proportion of zeros are simple (Conrey 1989). A multiple zero would be a point where ξ'(ρ) = 0, violating the implicit function theorem for R-curves. The Spectral Cannon's Persistence finding — |ζ'(ρ)| ~ √γ growing without bound — provides strong evidence against multiple zeros.
