**Existence and Uniqueness (from Symmetry).** The constraint function of each mechanism class is antisymmetric about the reflection axis (the functional equation forces this). An antisymmetric continuous function has exactly one zero on the axis. The derivative-level mechanism enumeration (§22.5) examines each class at the derivative level: the per-class reductions compile (C₁ derives), and the joint step — that no class produces a double zero — is the *geometric clause*, carried openly (§24.4), the derivative-level counterpart of the arithmetic clause that excludes off-line zeros. One zero at the symmetry axis; its simplicity is that geometric clause, not a local consequence here.
