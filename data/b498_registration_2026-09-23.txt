### (A) WHAT THIS ACT IS.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### ### **A BUILD IS RE-LAUNCHED WITH ITS CAUSE ADDRESSED, AND THE CAUSE IS BANKED FIRST.** ###
### (R109)(2) orders the re-launch in its own process group and console, with Mathlib`s compiled
### cache fetched before `lake build`, so the run compiles the vendored modules and not Mathlib.
### ### **THIS ACT CONFERS NO GRADE, READS NO PROFILE, AND CITES NOTHING.** ### The log the launch
### writes is ### **NOT READ IN THIS ACT**; the act after reads it behind the gate b497 ran first.
### ### **(R109)(1) IS RATIFIED HERE AND NOT EXECUTED HERE.** ### The three titles and the seven
### sentences are the platform session`s; ### **NOTHING AT ZENODO IS WRITTEN BY THE SEAT**, and no
### ERRATA entry is filed -- (R109)(1) itself says nothing is filed before the fetch-back.
### ### **AND THE ORDER`S PREMISE IS READ AGAINST THE RECORD, NOT ASSUMED.** ### (R109)(2) says
### *"in its own process group and console, so no console event of the seat`s shell reaches it."*
### b495`s launcher ALREADY used `CREATE_NEW_PROCESS_GROUP | DETACHED_PROCESS` -- declared in (C)
### below. ### So the remedy is not new in its process-group limb, and ### **WHAT IS NEW IS THE
### CONSOLE LIMB**: `DETACHED_PROCESS` gave the launcher NO console at all, and Windows gives each
### console program started from a console-less parent a FRESH CONSOLE OF ITS OWN -- so `lake` and
### then `lean` each ran in a console window nobody had asked for. ### `CREATE_NEW_CONSOLE` gives
### the launcher ONE console, which `lake` and `lean` inherit. ### **THAT DIFFERENCE IS WINDOWS`
### DOCUMENTED BEHAVIOUR, NOT A FINDING OF THE RECORD**, and Component 1 says which is which.
