WHY THESE THREE
================
The derived count for the pure lag form is NPOS(S_k) = (M - nullity)/2 with
M = q k + s and nullity = s (q even) or k - s (q odd). In the continuum r = M/k:

m/(2m+1) at r = 2m+1 (L = 8, 32, 128, ...) with values 1/3, 2/5, 3/7, ...
