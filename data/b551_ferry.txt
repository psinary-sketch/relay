strikeable: THE TOOLCHAINS ALIGNED — A FEDERATION TOOLCHAIN

TRIALLED ON A BRANCH; THE CASCADE PAUSES ONE ACT.

(1) WHY THIS ACT COMES BEFORE THE_KEYSTONE_CENSUS. b549
found residue_irreducible and h2_sign_iff_rh on different
Mathlib pins; b550 found the Interfaces pin of
SIDE-global-section incomplete on D: (one olean missing at
cecd0c4) and one module elaborating at a different checkout
alone; SPIRAL_MAP cites no pin for that repository. CP-1b
reads every citer of every moved terminal, and a citer that
cannot be elaborated at its own pin cannot be read as
compiled. So the toolchains are put in order once, as a
census with the facts printed, before the cascade continues.

(2) THE CENSUS. For every SIDE-* repository on D: (the 37
of b542 plus the eight of b546): the lean-toolchain line;
the Mathlib rev from lake-manifest (or VANILLA if none);
whether a checkout of that rev exists on D: and whether its
oleans are complete for the modules the repository imports
(tested by `lake env lean` on each root module, not by
directory listing); whether `lake build` succeeds from the
cache; and the tag or SHA at which SPIRAL_MAP pins it (or
NONE). One table, banked, every cell printed. Repositories
that are VANILLA are so marked and need no alignment.

(3) THE INTERFACES PIN IS COMPLETED, not substituted: the
missing olean at D:\mathlib4 cecd0c4 is built there (cost
printed before the build; background if over 600 s), and
all four Interfaces files of SIDE-global-section are then
elaborated at the declared pin with their prints compared
to the bank. If RestrictedTensorLayer1 does not elaborate at
cecd0c4, that is a defect of the file's declared pin,
printed, and the file's own v4.29.0 print stands as its
pin with a correspondence-row note; the pin is not moved to
make it pass.

(4) SIDE-GLOBAL-SECTION IS TAGGED v0.2.0 at 2e43315,
annotated, peeled SHA read back from the remote, as the
counterpart of (R160)(1); SPIRAL_MAP gains an appended row
with that pin; the missing correspondence row for
AggregationCircularityShadow is added through the
repository's committed writer, and the row numbering 81–89
is recorded at the IDENTITY_CHAIN tier block by appended
line.

(5) THE WEIL-SIDE ALIGNMENT IS TRIALLED, NOT MERGED. The
two kernels are SIDE-explicit-formula (v0.2 = 5c72cad) and
SIDE-lv-conservation (v0.11.0 = 2f71068). The census prints
their two Mathlib revs and the commit distance between
them. The direction is toward the newer rev. On a branch of
the older kernel named toolchain-trial-b551, the manifest is
moved to the newer rev and `lake build` is run once, with
the leading error per module printed and counted; no source
line is edited on the branch beyond the manifest and
toolchain files. The branch is pushed by name and HELD; main
is untouched. The result is the price of the alignment in
errors by module, entered under W-ORD-LI-WEIL-BRIDGE's
toolchain item at OPEN_TRAILS :11203. If the build is clean,
that is printed and the merge is still not done at this
act.

(6) THE_KEYSTONE_CENSUS is the act after this one.
