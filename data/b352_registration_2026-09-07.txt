### (D) THE FITTING CRITERION. ### ONE CRITERION FOR ALL THREE, FIXED HERE.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### ### **ALL THREE MODELS ARE FITTED BY MINIMISING THE SUM OF SQUARED RESIDUALS OF `log R`:**
### `S = sum_i (log R_i - log f(X_i))^2`.
### ### ### **WHY LOG AND NOT LINEAR, DECIDED BEFORE ANY FIT:** ### `R` spans about `1.7` orders across
### the ladder, so a linear-error fit is dominated by the single largest point at `X = 8` and says almost
### nothing about the last rung, which is exactly where a floor would show. ### **AND THERE IS A SECOND,
### ### DECISIVE REASON: b322's `fit_power`, WHICH THE RECORD ALREADY USED ON THIS OBJECT, IS A LOG-SPACE
### ### FIT.** ### Fitting `M2` and `M3` in linear space while `M1` came from a log-space fit would be a
### comparison of two different criteria wearing one name.
### ### **THE LIKE-FOR-LIKE FIXTURE, REQUIRED BEFORE ANY SCORE IS REPORTED:** ### this act's own `M1`
### must reproduce `b322_ladder.fit_power`'s `(p, A, rms)` at every cell, ### **THE FITTER IMPORTED AND
### ### NOT COPIED**, to `1e-9` relative. ### **IF IT DOES NOT, NO SCORE IS REPORTED AND THE ACT SAYS SO.**
### ### **THE SELECTION CRITERION: `AICc = n log(S/n) + 2k + 2k(k+1)/(n - k - 1)`**, with `n = 5` per cell.
### ### Lower is preferred. ### **AND WHERE `n - k - 1 <= 0` THE MODEL IS MARKED `NOT SCOREABLE ON THIS
### ### LADDER` AND IS NOT RANKED**, which for `n = 5` is every model with four or more parameters.

### (E) THE BARS. ### EACH WITH THE FLOOR OF THE OBJECT IT TESTS. ### THE BAR-FLOOR RULE, MINTED AT b347.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### ### **THE OBJECT'S FLOOR, MEASURED BEFORE THIS SEAL AND NOT ASSUMED:** ### `R = margin - int_ef`, and
### `int_ef` enters b339's record with ### **`9` DECIMAL PLACES AT ALL THREE CELLS**, so `R` is known to
### ### **`5e-10` ABSOLUTE** however many digits the JSON prints. ### The smallest residual anywhere on the
### ladder is `1.880778106e-02`, so the ### **RELATIVE FLOOR AT THE LAST RUNG IS `2.658e-08`.**
### ### **BAR 1 -- DISTINGUISHABLE BY SCORE.** ### Two scoreable models are DISTINGUISHABLE when their
### `AICc` differ by more than `2.0`.
###   ### **ITS FLOOR:** ### an `AICc` difference is driven by `n log(S/n)`; with `n = 5` and the relative
###   ### floor `2.658e-08`, the smallest `S` distinguishable from zero is about `5 x (2.658e-08)^2 =
###   ### 3.5e-15`, and `AICc` differences at that scale are far below `2.0`. ### **THE BAR SITS ABOVE ITS
###   ### OBJECT'S FLOOR BY MANY ORDERS, AND IS THEREFORE STRICT RATHER THAN UNINFORMATIVE.**
### ### **BAR 2 -- A POSITIVE FLOOR, AS A STATEMENT ABOUT ONE PARAMETER.** ### `M2`'s fitted `c` counts as
### a POSITIVE FLOOR only when ### **`c > 0` AND `c` EXCEEDS THE M2 FIT'S OWN SCATTER AT THE LAST RUNG**,
### that scatter being `rms(M2) x R(128)` in absolute terms.
###   ### **ITS FLOOR:** ### `5e-10` absolute, as above. ### A bar on `c` below `5e-10` would be a bar on
###   ### the printing of `int_ef` and not on the residual.
### ### ### **WHAT MAKES BAR 1 AND BAR 2 INDEPENDENT, NAMED AS b347's RULE REQUIRES:** ### Bar 1 ranks
### ### WHOLE MODELS by total fit across five points; Bar 2 asks whether ONE PARAMETER of ONE model is
### ### positive and larger than that model's own scatter. ### **THEY CAN DISAGREE, AND THE CASE WHERE
### ### THEY DO IS THE INFORMATIVE ONE:** `M2` may score best while its `c` comes out negative, which is
### ### a better fit achieved by an unphysical floor and is evidence AGAINST a floor, not for one. ### An
### ### act that ran only Bar 1 could report "the floor model wins" in exactly that case.
### ### **THIS IS A TWO-ARM BAR AND IS NOT MARKED `SINGLE-ARM`**, and the independence above is what the
### rule demands be named.
