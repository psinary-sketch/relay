<p>THE CLAIM. A PLACE TO STAND is the name of the research programme whose papers, kernels and ledgers this record deposits; it makes its claim in one sentence, as its README states it: "RH reduced to a single located clause, reduction machine-verified." Here RH is the Riemann Hypothesis, as Mathlib states it (RiemannHypothesis): every zero s of the Riemann zeta function that is not a trivial zero −2(n+1) and is not s = 1 has real part 1/2. The located clause: the Riemann Hypothesis holds exactly when the explicit-formula quadratic form Q(k) = pole term − prime sum + archimedean term is non-negative for every admissible test window k, the theorem h2_sign_iff_rh deriving the equivalence with no premise, and the open clause being the sign statement h2_sign itself, which the kernel states, proves equivalent to RH, bounds by its mechanism exclusions, and carries as its one open premise. Its mechanism exclusions are the route terminals of SIDE-kernel, which compile that none of seven named mechanism classes produces the off-line signature the kernel defines, the exhaustiveness of the seven classes over all mechanisms not being compiled; they rule out named sources of a zero off the critical line and do not prove h2_sign. h2_sign is Weil positivity on classK: for every test window k in classK, 0 ≤ P(k) − PR(k) + A(k), the pole term, the prime sum and the archimedean term of the explicit formula; open, and carried as the one open premise. h2_sign is the one open clause of the reduction: the dependency path from h2_sign to RiemannHypothesis passes through the seam rh_strip_imp_rh, a classical fact the kernel compiles (rh_strip_imp_rh_holds), and consumes no OPEN premise; the OPEN premises in the table belong to the faces, instances and bounds, none on that path [in plain words: every OPEN premise in the table is assumed only by theorems the reduction from h2_sign to RH does not use], and the table is the keystone census at v0.7.1, in this record's files. classK is the admissible test windows: functions k on the reals that are even, twice continuously differentiable and compactly supported, and of the form h ⋆ h~ with h twice continuously differentiable and compactly supported. The ceiling, in the README's words: supportable, "RH reduced to a single located clause, reduction machine-verified"; not supported, "RH proved" -- the corpus does not support that sentence, since h2_sign is open, and nothing in this record states that the Riemann Hypothesis holds.</p><p>WHAT IS MACHINE-VERIFIED.
