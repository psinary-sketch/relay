### (C) PART (a): THE RATIO, ITS DENOMINATOR, AND ITS FLOOR. ### [SEAT, PRE-RUN]
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### ### **THE OBJECT.** ### At each charted aim the record holds an archimedean term and a prime-side term
### of comparable size, and the room is their difference. ### In the banked rows these are `arch_z` and
### `prime_z`, with `places_z` their difference, and the same three for the `q` side.
### ### ### **THE RATIO, FIXED HERE AND BEFORE ANY VALUE:**
### ### ### **`R_rel = |places| / max(|arch|, |prime|)`.**
### ### **WHY THAT DENOMINATOR AND NOT ANOTHER, STATED BEFORE THE FIGURES ARE SEEN:** it is the LARGER of
### the two terms the room sits between, so the ratio cannot be inflated by a small denominator; a mean or
### a minimum would each be smaller and would each make every room look wider. ### **THE CONSERVATIVE
### ### CHOICE IS THE ONE THAT CANNOT FLATTER THE ACT'S OWN QUESTION.** ### The same denominator is used at
### every aim, at both widths, and on both sides; ### **NO AIM GETS ITS OWN DENOMINATOR.**
### ### ### **THE FLOOR OF THE OBJECT THIS RATIO IS BUILT ON, AS THE BAR-FLOOR RULE (b347) REQUIRES.**
### ### The numerator's floor is the floor of `places` itself, and the record states it per row: each row
### carries the noise-floor gate's own verdict on `places`, with its drift, and `noise_floor.DEFAULT_FLOOR`
### is `sqrt` of machine epsilon, `1.4901161193847656e-08`. ### **SO A ROOM BELOW THAT FLOOR IS NOT A
### ### SMALL ROOM, IT IS NO MEASUREMENT**, and any row whose gate does not return `RESOLVED` on its
### `places` value is ### **EXCLUDED FROM THE MINIMUM AND REPORTED AS EXCLUDED.** ### The denominator's
### floor is the same, and it is never near it -- the terms are of order `1e-1` -- so the ratio's floor is
### the numerator's.
### ### ### **AND THE RATIO ITSELF CARRIES NO SEALED TOLERANCE. ### THERE IS NO THRESHOLD ON `R_rel`, NO
### ### VALUE OF IT PASSES OR FAILS ANYTHING, AND SO THERE IS NOTHING HERE TO PRICE: `UNPRICED` WOULD BE
### ### THE WRONG WORD BECAUSE NO BAR IS SEALED ON IT AT ALL.** ### The only bar in part (a) is the
### imported gate's, whose floor is stated above.
