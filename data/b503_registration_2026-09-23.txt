### (B) THE COMPONENTS, AS THE ORDER PUTS THEM -- COMPONENT (1) THROUGH COMPONENT (4).
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### (1) THE FAILURE, LOCATED. ### The chain`s own bank, `carto_atlas.GAM` -- its count and its top
###     ordinate printed from the file -- with `b326_closure.json` named as a different bank. ### At
###     each of the 23 new unverified cells: the image `2 pi / dv` (dv from the cell`s own L and the
###     grid of `2 x 8193 - 1` points), `trunc` and `|W + Z|` from b502`s bank, and the kind.
### (2) THE ZERO SIDE, ALIAS-FREE. ### `Z = 2 SUM hhat_exact(gamma)` over the same 10,000 ordinates, by
###     `b326_closure.hhat_exact` (imported, unedited), at all 119 cells, on the same `(v, w)` the chain
###     formed. ### **THE ZERO BANK IS UNCHANGED.**
###     ### ### **THE FIXTURE, AND THE READING IT NEEDS.** ### The ferry orders the exact transform to
###     *"reproduce the trapezoid value to 1e-12"*. ### Read literally that bar is below the floor of
###     the comparison: the trapezoid sum and the exact transform of the same piecewise-linear `w` are
###     NOT the same number -- away from the alias images they are related ### **EXACTLY** by
###     `exact(u) = sinc^2(u dv / 2) x trapezoid(u)`, and at a zero ordinate the two differ by a
###     relative `(gamma dv)^2 / 12`, about 1e-6. ### So the fixture is taken, at one verified cell
###     (a = 4.061553) and at the lowest five ordinates, ON THAT IDENTITY, held to the order`s 1e-12
###     relative; ### **THE RAW DIFFERENCE IS PRINTED BESIDE IT**, so the reader sees both. ### The
###     bar is kept; what it is applied to is the relation the two transforms actually satisfy.
###     ### ### **THE RESIDUAL, AS ORDERED, AND ONE BESIDE IT.** ### The ORDERED residual is
###     `Z_exact - (P - PR + A)` with P, PR and A the chain`s own as banked (A by the trapezoid transform
###     on `|u| <= 600`); ### **VERIFICATION IS SCORED ON IT.** ### Beside it, and not in place of it,
###     a CONSISTENT residual with A re-formed by `hhat_exact` on the same u-grid, so that both sides
###     use one transform of one `f` -- b326`s own lesson -- printed at every cell.
###     ### ### **THE HALT RULE:** any cell whose ordered residual still exceeds its bound stays
###     ### **UNVERIFIED** and is listed with its kind; nothing is re-bounded to pass it. ### For each
###     ESTIMATE-SHORT cell, the exact residual beside b501`s estimate and beside the trapezoid
###     residual, so the act says whether the shortfall was the transform`s or the estimate`s.
### (3) THE TAIL, ON VERIFIED CELLS ALONE. ### The extrema of m(a); b502`s four one-parameter fits
###     re-scored by the same loss (RMS absolute residual) on the verified old and the verified new
###     cells; whether the rise after a = 13.153 survives. ### m(a) itself is the places side and is
###     not changed by this act; ### **WHAT CHANGES IS WHICH CELLS MAY BE READ.**
### (4) THE ATTRIBUTION. ### At every verified cell: m(a), Z(a) (exact), the pole term P, and
###     `m - Z` printed; Z split zero by zero -- `2 hhat_exact(gamma_k)` for the lowest five ordinates of
###     the bank, and the remainder. ### For each extremum of m(a), the zero-terms that have their own
###     extremum within one cell of it (adjacent in the verified ordering), ### **OR NONE.**
