b681 -- THE RULES BANKED BY THIS ACT (2026-10-18)

### rows are rules, each with the ruling that makes it and what it governs (b673's form, `  (n) ...`).

  (1) A ROOT WHOSE NAMED BANKS STOOD RE-BANKED AFTER ITS RECORDED COMMIT IS REPAIRED BY A CORRIGENDUM ROW. Its form, appended to
      data/act_roots.txt with no row edited: "corrigendum <act> <recorded commit> <corrected commit> <root> <bank> ...", the root value as the
      act recorded it and the banks named those whose recorded hash the corrected commit carries. Not a link of the chain: tools/act_root.py's
      last_root and the chain read past it, and verify reads the banks it names at the corrected commit only when the row's act has a chain
      row, its recorded commit is that act's, its root is that act's, the corrected commit descends from the recorded one, and every bank it
      names is one the act's root names. Where any named bank differs at the corrected commit, no row is written and the author is asked.
      (R291)(2); first applied at b681 to b680 (data/b681_root_repair.txt).
  (2) A PROMPT AFTER THE SEAL IS THE ROOT'S ALONE, AND ITS PRINTS GO WHERE THE POST-ROOT PRINTS GO. A prompt raised after the seal is the
      root prompt alone; its answer and any defect it enters go to the defects bank and to no bank the root names. The suite's count arms
      (G-ANSWERS-BANKED, G-ANSWERS-CHECKED, G-DEFECTS-LETTERED) read their counts from the banks at HEAD with the sealed figure as a floor,
      and any post-seal prompt is named on the face. (R291)(3).
  (3) EVERY BANK A ROOT NAMES IS COMMITTED AND PUSHED AT THE COMMIT THE ROOT RECORDS. At root time every bank the root route names stands
      committed, the commit the root records is the HEAD at which every named bank stands, git status prints clean for the named list, and
      the remote main is read equal to HEAD before the root is computed; the reading banked as data/<act>_root_banks.txt, and the standing arm
      G-ROOT-BANKS-COMMITTED reads it. (R291)(4); the b680 lesson (its defect (h)).
