RULING (R153), the author's, ratified by this paste and
strikeable: THE EDITORIAL PHASE — ITS VOICE, ITS CHECKPOINTS,
AND THE MONOGRAPH READ'S SECOND ACT.

(1) THE PHASE. The programme is in an editorial phase: every
fact at every layer — kernel statement, Correspondence row,
keystone sentence, deposited sentence — is corroborated
before anything goes further before others. No deposit, no
circulation, no v6 until the checkpoints of (4) have fired.

(2) THE VOICE. The editorial voice is descriptive: a
sentence says what a theorem states and what its proof
consumes, in words. Counts live in banks and tables, and a
count enters a sentence when the count is the finding (the
writing law). A grade is not a verdict on a person or an
era; it is a relation between a terminal and a claim (R40).
Sentences that describe a past state — Chapter 27.1's
correction record, the version log, dated era annotations,
DELTA blocks — are read as records of their date and graded
STANDS-AS-HISTORY when accurate to that date; they are not
RESTS rows and they do not draw errata. This stops the
correction treadmill on history.

(3) THE POSTURE AGAINST RECENCY. Earlier acts' grades are
inputs, not verdicts: when act two of the read meets a
terminal graded at b539–b542, the seat re-reads the
terminal's statement at its pin before carrying the grade,
and prints CARRIED or MOVED for each. Expectations are
refutable by printed lines. Nothing is trusted from recall,
the navigator's included.

(4) THE CHECKPOINTS, each producing a bank and a FINDINGS
entry, none a new document, in this order unless the author
re-orders:
CP-1 The cascade closes — every graded Correspondence table
tiered, the monograph read whole (this act closes its
second half), the Phase 1.2 presentations and the six
companion papers read when mirrored; no blank tier cell.
CP-2 The T0 inventory — every terminal in the federation
whose statement is against Mathlib's objects at the standard
three with no premise, once, with its statement and its
subject (about zeta; about logic or arithmetic; about a
programme-defined type), generated from b542's enumeration
of 5,293 declarations.
CP-3 The inequality index — every compiled inequality about
zeta or about test windows, marked I, with what it forces;
every equality-shaped step marked E. The E/I mark is the
editorial phase's instrument for telling a step that moves a
zero from one that cannot.
CP-4 The page without narrative — the tier counts, the
inheritance and register censuses, the deposit reads'
dispositions, CP-2's subjects, CP-3's index, the fixture
banks' verified counts, on one page with no prose; the
ceiling sentence is its last line and is derived from it.
CP-5 Deposit reconciliation closed — every deposited sentence
read, errata filed, descriptions current, the project's own
description block brought to the ceiling by the author.
CP-6 Phase 2 inheritance — each Phase 2 keystone's table
states its own T0 content and that it inherits nothing from
Phase 1 (the README's rule), by appended row.
CP-7 The observation document's purpose statement, approved
by the author before any body text; its working name is the
author's; its title names its object; it is Tier K with a
Correspondence table; it is written from CP-1–CP-6's banks
and from nothing else.
CP-8 v6 of the monograph, last, from the chapter tier map.
The act after this one takes CP-2 and CP-3 together (both
from b542's enumeration); BALANCE_AND_POSITIVITY follows.

(5) FINDINGS.md:1109 stays as written; one appended note
says the declaration it names is TECHNE-Core's, private, and
is not cited elsewhere; nothing is deleted.

(6) The local branches push-b542 and push-b542-closing are
deleted by name after `git branch --merged main` lists them.
