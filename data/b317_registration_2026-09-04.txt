### (5) THE ASSEMBLY AND THE FRAMES, DECLARED BEFORE THEY RUN.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### ### **THE ASSEMBLY, FROM THE SOURCE'S DEFINITIONS AND NOTHING ELSE.** ### eq. (61) gives the
### scaling action; integrating it against a test function in the multiplicative group's own measure
### and substituting `u = x/lambda` gives an integral operator with kernel
### ### **`K(x,u) = f(x/u) / sqrt(x u)`**; the compression by b316's projector and the trace then
### give ### **`Tr(theta(f) S) = Tr(A_HH) - Tr(Q^T A_HH Q)`**, which needs the diagonal and `rank`
### matrix-vector products and never forms the operator whole.
### ### **THE SUBSTITUTION IS CHECKED BY TWO INDEPENDENT CODE PATHS**, one building the kernel and
### one integrating the instrument's own `scaling` over `v = log lambda`, and the fixture requires
### the agreement AND requires it to break under a deliberately halved kernel.
### ### **THE POSITIVE CONTROL THE ORDER NAMES:** ### a scaling by the identity alone recovers the
### projection's trace, which is the truncation's dimension. ### It runs through the same two terms.

### ### **THE TWO TEST FUNCTIONS, IN THE SOURCE'S OWN VARIABLE `v = log rho`.**
###   ### **(T1) THE CORPUS'S INTEGRAL-ONE BUMP**, taken from the corpus's own emitting file
###     `tools/e16/carto_atlas.py::bump` and not rewritten; support `[1/a, a]`.
###   ### **(T2) A MEAN-ZERO VARIANT** built from three of that same bump at widths `a`, `a^(1/2)`,
###     `a^(1/4)`, with the coefficients solved so that BOTH of the source's moments vanish: the
###     transform at zero `INT f d*rho = 0`, and ### **THE SOURCE'S OWN eq. (54)**,
###     `INT f(rho) rho^{+-1/2} d*rho = 0`, which for an even test function is one condition.
###     Scale fixed by `INT |f| dv = 1`, which is the same norm (T1) carries.
### ### **THE CLASS BOUNDARY IS REPORTED AS A MEASUREMENT, NOT ASSERTED:** ### both moments are
### printed for both functions, and the source's eq. (53) support condition -- `supp f` inside
### `[1/2, 2]` -- is printed per cell, so the cells that leave it are named. ### **eq. (53)'s SUPPORT
### ### CONDITION AND eq. (54)'s VANISHING CONDITIONS ARE THE SOURCE'S CONDITIONS ON `W_infinity`;
### ### THEOREM 4.7's OWN STATEMENT CARRIES NEITHER**, and that distinction is quoted rather than
### smoothed over.

### ### **THE FRAMES, FIXED HERE.** ### `NY = 512` throughout, so the two axes move one thing each.
###   ### **GRID AXIS** (`X = 32`): ### `N = 2048, 4096, 8192, 16384`, i.e. `h = 1/64 ... 1/512`.
###   ### **DOMAIN AXIS** (`h = 1/128`): ### `X = 8, 16, 32, 64, 128`.
###   ### **THE SHARED CELL IS `N = 4096, X = 32`**, and it is the reference frame for the
