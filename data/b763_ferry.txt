## XXV. A statement's position (added <DATE>, b763, under the author's word of 2026-11-19, `(R373)`(9); beside XXIV)

A paper says where a statement stands by its relations to fixed points, and names each relation by the declaration or the source that carries it. The positions: DERIVED, proved from Mathlib with the three standard axioms; EQUIVALENT TO THE HYPOTHESIS, both directions compiled; IMPLIES THE HYPOTHESIS, that direction compiled; FOLLOWS FROM THE HYPOTHESIS, that direction compiled; PROVED ON A RANGE, a theorem up to a stated bound; FAILS AT THE CONTROL, compiled false at the Epstein zeta function of discriminant -23; A HYPOTHESIS BY DESIGN, part of what a theorem is about; CARRIED FROM A SOURCE, proved elsewhere and cited at a pin. A statement may hold several, and a paper gives each that it holds: the combination is the finding.

The word open, standing alone, gives no position. At each paper's next edition it gives way to the positions the statement holds.

Worked at the centre. Weil positivity on classK is EQUIVALENT TO THE HYPOTHESIS (`h2_sign_iff_rh`) and FAILS AT THE CONTROL (`zq_weil_positivity_fails`). It holds at a width L when it holds on each window that vanishes outside [-L, L]; holding at a width, it holds at each narrower one, so the widths at which it holds have one upper end, its critical width. The Hypothesis is the statement that zeta's critical width is infinite. A width at which the form is proved at or above 0, or compiled below 0, is a position of the kind PROVED ON A RANGE, entered for zeta when a source for that width is read at content, and for the control when a width is compiled.
