### (C) THE UNITS OF THE ANSWER, FIXED IN ADVANCE, SO THAT NO COORDINATE IS GRADED BY WHAT IT RETURNS.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### For each of the four coordinates this act reports exactly three things, in this order:
###   ### **(C1) THE RANGE THE RECORD HAS CHARTED**, as a quotation at an emitting line.
###   ### **(C2) WHETHER THE RECORD CAN BOUND THE COORDINATE**, in one of exactly three states, fixed
###   ### here:
###       ### `BOUNDED BY AN ARGUMENT` -- the record contains a statement that closes the coordinate for
###       ### ALL values, not merely for the values tried. ### **THIS IS THE ONLY STATE THAT BOUNDS AN
###       ### OBJECT RATHER THAN AN INSTRUMENT.**
###       ### `BOUNDED BY A MEASUREMENT` -- the record has looked as far as it has looked and says so;
###       ### the bound is on the LOOKING, not on the coordinate.
###       ### `NOT BOUNDED` -- neither is present.
###   ### **(C3) WHERE A CLASS WOULD HAVE TO BE PROVED SILENT RATHER THAN MEASURED**, named as a class,
###   ### or the words `NO SUCH CLASS` where the coordinate is closed by argument.
### ### ### **AND ONE DISTINCTION IS FIXED HERE BECAUSE IT IS THE WHOLE HAZARD OF THIS ACT:**
### ### ### **A BOUND ON THE INSTRUMENT IS NOT A BOUND ON THE COORDINATE.** ### An evaluator that stops
### ### ### at a radius, a library that stops at a height, a frame that stops at a support -- each of
### ### ### these bounds WHAT CAN BE SEEN and says nothing whatever about what is there. ### An act that
### ### ### reports an instrument's ceiling as a coordinate's range has confused the two, and this
### ### ### registration fixes the words that keep them apart before any coordinate is read.
### ### **AND A SECOND, FIXED BEFORE THE HEIGHT IS READ:** ### **A METHOD THAT PRODUCES INSTANCES DOES
### ### NOT PRODUCE A CLASSIFICATION.** ### Running a census higher yields more zeros; it never yields a
### statement about all zeros above a height. ### If the record's only method on a coordinate is of that
### kind, then the coordinate's price is a price for the wrong object, and this act must say so rather
### than print the price and let it read as progress.
