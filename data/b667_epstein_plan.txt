== 2. THE EVALUATION ROUTE (RAISED): THE CHOWLA-SELBERG FORM OF Z_Q, EVERY TERM AN ARB BALL OR A STATED BOUND
  Q(m, n) = (m + n/2)² + (23/4) n². The row n = 0 gives 2ζ(2s). For n ≠ 0, Poisson summation in m of f(x) = ((x + α)² + β²)^{−s} with
  α = n/2, β = β_n = √23 |n| / 2, whose Fourier transform is √π Γ(s − ½)/Γ(s) β^{1−2s} at ξ = 0 (the beta integral) and
  (2π^s/Γ(s)) |ξ|^{s−½} β^{½−s} K_{s−½}(2π|ξ|β) at ξ ≠ 0 (DLMF 10.32.11 with ν = s − ½, z = β, x = 2πξ); e^{2πikα} = (−1)^{kn};
  Σ_{n ≠ 0} β_n^{1−2s} = 2 (√23/2)^{1−2s} ζ(2s − 1); the k- and n-sums even in sign. Hence, for Re s > 1 and then on C \ {1} by analytic
  continuation (the double sum converges absolutely and uniformly on compacta, K decaying as e^{−x}):
      Z_Q(s) = T1 + T2 + T3 + T4
      T1 = 2 ζ(2s)
      T2 = 2 √π Γ(s − ½) Γ(s)⁻¹ (√23/2)^{1−2s} ζ(2s − 1)
      T3 = 8 π^s Γ(s)⁻¹ Σ_{k, n ≥ 1, kn ≤ M} (−1)^{kn} k^{s−½} β_n^{½−s} K_{s−½}(π√23 kn)
      T4 = the same sum over kn > M (the truncation), bounded and added to the ball as its radius.
  The formula: S. Chowla and A. Selberg, On Epstein's zeta-function, J. reine angew. Math. 227 (1967) 86-110; derived above line by line so
  the act rests on the derivation, not on the citation alone. T1 and T2 each have a pole at s = ½ which cancels; every point the act
  evaluates has Im s ≥ 0.5, so no pole is met.

  THE TERMS, THEIR ERROR BOUNDS AND THEIR SOURCES (Arb returns a ball containing the true value for every function named; python-flint 0.9.0,
  the docstrings read at step zero, relay data/b667_readfirst.txt):
    T1     acb.zeta (Arb's acb_zeta)                                        the ball Arb returns
    T2     acb.gamma (Γ(s − ½)), acb.rgamma (1/Γ(s)), acb.zeta (ζ(2s − 1)),  the balls Arb returns, multiplied in ball arithmetic
           acb powers (acb_pow, principal branch; the bases positive reals)
    T3     acb.bessel_k (Arb's acb_hypgeom_bessel_k, complex order),         the balls Arb returns; M terms at most Σ_{m ≤ M} d(m) Bessel
           acb.rgamma, acb powers, arb.pi                                     calls
    T4     BOUNDED HERE, elementary:                                         an upper bound, added as the radius
             |K_ν(x)| ≤ K_{Re ν}(x) for x > 0 (DLMF 10.32.9: K_ν(x) = ∫_0^∞ e^{−x cosh u} cosh(νu) du, and |cosh(νu)| ≤ cosh(Re ν · u));
             for |Re ν| ≤ 1 (here Re ν = σ − ½ ∈ [−1, 1] on the whole region): K_{Re ν}(x) ≤ ∫_0^∞ e^{−x(1 + u²/2)} e^{u} du
               ≤ e^{−x} √(2π/x) e^{1/(2x)} (cosh u ≥ 1 + u²/2; cosh(au) ≤ e^{u} for |a| ≤ 1; the square completed);
             |k^{s−½} β_n^{½−s}| = (k/β_n)^{σ−½} ≤ (√23/2)^{|σ−½|} m^{|σ−½|} for kn = m (k, n ≤ m, β_n ≥ √23/2);
             the pairs with kn = m number d(m) ≤ 2√m; x = π√23 m;
             so |T4| ≤ 8 |π^s/Γ(s)| (√23/2)^{a} · 2 √(2/√23) e^{1/(2π√23)} · Σ_{m > M} m^{a} e^{−π√23 m}, a = |σ − ½| ≤ 1,
             and Σ_{m > M} m^{a} e^{−cm} ≤ (M + 1)^{a} e^{−c(M+1)} / (1 − 2 e^{−c}), c = π√23 (successive ratios ≤ 2e^{−c}).
           Computed in arb arithmetic, its upper end taken; |π^s/Γ(s)| from acb.rgamma. On a segment ball the bound is taken at the ball
           (σ's upper value in a, the ball's |π^s/Γ(s)|).

== 5. THE CONTOUR'S DISCRETISATION AND THE ON-CONTOUR EXCLUSION
  A segment [z_a, z_b] is enclosed by the ball centred at its midpoint with radius half its length along its direction; F evaluated on that
  ball (the route of section 2 with ball input) encloses F on the whole segment. The segment is ACCEPTED when the enclosure excludes 0 with
  its radius at most half its midpoint's modulus -- then F has no zero on the segment and arg F varies there by less than π/3 about the
  midpoint's argument, so the segment's argument change is the principal arg(F(z_b)/F(z_a)) from the endpoint balls. Otherwise the segment
  is bisected, to depth 16 on a square and 24 on P; at the depth limit, the 400-bit rung once; still not accepted, the segment is printed
  UNRESOLVED with its location and that rectangle's count is not certified. The accepted segments cover the contour, so F ≠ 0 on it is
  proved, not assumed.
  Initial segments: 4 per side of a square; on P, 8 on the bottom edge, 299 on the right edge (step 0.5, the census's own box step), 8 on the
  top edge. A square's count ball is (1/2π) Σ Δ_j; on R, section 4's N(R).
