----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### ### **THE AXIS IS RAISED FROM `NY = 512` TO `NY = 1024`.**
### ### **AND `1024` IS NOT AN INVENTED NUMBER:** ### it is a rung of `b344`'s own quadrature ladder,
### already run by that act at a different frame. ### **THE ACT DID NOT PICK A VALUE; IT PICKED A VALUE THE
### ### RECORD HAS ALREADY USED.**
### ### **THE HEADROOM:** ### `b354` measured the sixth rung's rank at `512` against a bound of `512`, with
### the banked ranks `20, 37, 69, 133, 262` extrapolating to about `516`. ### At `1024` the bound is about
### twice that estimate. ### **AN ESTIMATE IS NOT A GUARANTEE**, and section (F)'s third verdict exists for
### the case where the raise does not clear the bound.
### ### **AND RAISING IT IS RE-TUNING, SAID PLAINLY.** ### Every act since `b316` has held `NY` fixed, and
### `b316`'s header gives the reason: the first version tied the constraint count to `1/h`, so that *"the
### second condition gets weaker and weaker as the domain lengthens"*, and at `X = 64` it was weak enough
### to admit a vector the corpus had derived is not in the space. ### **THE RAISE STRENGTHENS THAT
### ### CONDITION AND WEAKENS NOTHING** -- and it still makes the raised frame a different frame.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### ### ### **THE FLOOR OF THIS AXIS IS IN THE RECORD, AND IT WAS READ BEFORE THIS SEAL.** ### `b344` is
### the ONLY act that moved the quadrature axis, at a fixed frame `N = 4096, X = 32`, cell `a = 1.41`. ###
### Its trace along that ladder moves by ### **`1.754e-03`, `3.990e-04`, `9.753e-05`, `2.425e-05`** ###
### relative, rung to rung -- converging. ### **THE STEP THAT MATTERS, `512` TO `1024`, MOVES IT BY
### ### `9.753e-05` RELATIVE.** ### And `b344`'s rank is ### **CONSTANT AT `69` ACROSS THE WHOLE LADDER**,
### in its own words: *"AND THE RANK DOES NOT MOVE AT ALL"* and *"The constraint's effective rank is
### intrinsic across four doublings of the rows that sample it."*
### ### **BAR 1 -- THE CONTROL'S RANK.** ### The fifth rung's rank under the raised axis must equal its
### banked value, `262`, ### **EXACTLY.**
###   ### **ITS FLOOR:** ### none is needed and none is invented. ### A rank is an integer and the bar is
###   an equality, not a threshold. ### **THE PRECEDENT IS b344's:** the rank did not move across four
###   doublings of the same axis at a frame far from the bound.
### ### **BAR 2 -- THE CONTROL'S TRACE.** ### The fifth rung's trace under the raised axis must reproduce
### `b320`'s banked value to ### **`1e-3` RELATIVE.**
###   ### **ITS FLOOR:** ### `9.753e-05` relative, `b344`'s own measured move of this axis over exactly the
###   step this act takes. ### **THE BAR SITS AN ORDER ABOVE ITS FLOOR AND IS THEREFORE STRICT RATHER THAN
###   ### UNINFORMATIVE** -- and a bar TIGHTER than the floor would fail on a correct computation, which is
###   the defect `b347` minted its rule against.
###   ### **AND WHAT THE FLOOR IS NOT:** ### `b344` measured it at rank `69` against a bound of `512`, far
###   from the bound; the fifth rung sits at rank `262` against the same bound, closer. ### **THE FLOOR IS
###   ### A COMPARABLE-SCALE MEASUREMENT OF THIS AXIS AND NOT A MEASUREMENT OF THIS RUNG**, and the act
###   says so wherever it uses it.
### ### **BAR 3 -- THE IDENTITY CONTROL**, at both frames: `|Tr - dim| <= 1e-9` absolute.
###   ### **ITS FLOOR:** ### `dim * eps`, about `7e-12` at these dimensions. ### The bar sits two orders
###   above it.
### ### **BARS 1 AND 2 ARE INDEPENDENT, AND WHAT MAKES THEM SO IS NAMED:** ### bar 1 is an integer property
### of the cut and needs no arithmetic on the trace; bar 2 is a floating-point comparison of one number to
### another act's number. ### **THEY CAN DISAGREE, AND THE INFORMATIVE CASE IS BAR 1 FAILING WHILE BAR 2
### ### HOLDS:** a rank that moved while the trace barely did would say the cut had changed shape without
### changing its value much, which is exactly the confound this act exists to rule out.

### (E) THE CEILING. ### **CHOSEN, NOT READ, AND THE ORDER'S ADDITION (i) REQUIRES THAT SAID FIRST.**
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### ### ### **THIS ACT CANNOT PRICE ITS OWN FRAME FROM THE RECORD.**
### ### **WHAT IT CAN READ:** ### `b354` measured `212.1` seconds for the whole of its component, of which
### the decomposition was `8.1` and the three cells about `52` each. ### **THAT IS A READ WALL, AND IT IS
### ### THE FIRST THIS LADDER HAS.**
### ### **WHAT IT CANNOT READ:** ### the decomposition is `O(NY^2 * free)`, so doubling the axis is about
### FOUR TIMES that `8.1` seconds -- a shape, and a reliable one. ### **BUT THE CELLS DOMINATED THE WALL,
### ### AND HOW A CELL'S COST SCALES WITH A LARGER RANK IS NOT IN THE RECORD.** ### `b354` measured `52`
### seconds per cell at rank `512`; nothing says what it is at a larger one.
### ### ### **SO THE CEILING IS CHOSEN: `1800` SECONDS OF WALL AND `8` GIGABYTES.** ### Its basis is
### `b354`'s measured `212.1` seconds, a four-fold shape on the part of it that IS priceable, and ### **AN
### ### UNPRICEABLE FACTOR ON THE PART THAT DOMINATED IT.** ### **A CHOSEN CEILING IS NOT A READ ONE**, and
### if the run lands inside it that says the run beat a number this seat picked.
### ### **AND IF IT OVERRUNS:** ### the run is ABANDONED, the overrun is reported as ### **THE FINDING**,
### no bar moves, no partial value is taken, and the stopped output is banked as it stood.
