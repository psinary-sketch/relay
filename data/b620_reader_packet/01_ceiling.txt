The ceiling: what the programme`s documents may say of the Riemann Hypothesis, and what they may not (README.md, lines 106 to 111).

Supportable: *RH reduced to a single located clause, reduction machine-verified.*
Not supportable: *RH proved.* The open clause is a premise of the multiplicative
structure, undischarged, and the corpus names it openly at every site that depends
on it. Its residue coincides, object for object, with the number-field shadow of
Weil positivity — the century-old obstruction — which is evidence the localization
is faithful, not that it has been crossed.
