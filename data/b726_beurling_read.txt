### Three public arXiv papers read in their HTML versions (their PDF fetches returned compressed binary), the quotations as read; plus the record's own row. Read for one
### question: does any Beurling generalized-prime system carry a functional equation s -> a - s and a known zero off that equation's centre line? The coverage is printed per
### paper. Two searches found these; a search is not a survey of the field, and "no instance found" means found in these sources.

## (1) Broucke and Debruyne, On zero-density estimates and the PNT in short intervals for Beurling generalized numbers, arXiv:2211.08716v2 (Acta Arith. 207 (2023)), read whole
  ON A FUNCTIONAL EQUATION, its only sentence (Introduction): "where there is in general no additive structure or functional equation."
  ITS EXPLICIT SYSTEM (Appendix A), 1/2 < theta < beta < 1, epsilon > 0: "zeta has no zeros on the half-plane Re s > beta"; "rho_k = beta + i gamma_k"; "Therefore zeta(s) admits zeros at rho_k."
  THE LINE: Re s = β, the paper's parameter line, not the centre line of any functional equation.

## (2) Broucke, On the connection between zero-free regions and the error term in the Prime Number Theorem, arXiv:2507.13780v1, about 118,000 of 157,000 characters read verbatim
  ON A FUNCTIONAL EQUATION: none in the part read. Zeros on a prescribed contour: "zeta_P(s) has infinitely many zeros on the contour sigma = 1 - f(log|t|), and none to the right of this contour."

## (3) Hilberdink and Lapidus, Beurling Zeta Functions, Generalised Primes, and Fractal Membranes, arXiv:math/0410270v1, about 78,800 of 96,900 characters read verbatim
  ON A FUNCTIONAL EQUATION: it characterises when one exists by a criterion (Theorem 3.2), and says: "We do not give a complete answer to the latter difficult question, but indicate several approaches and give a criterion"
  for the existence of such a functional equation. A generalised equation for a general system is reported from a work in preparation, connecting a system to a dual system; no system with a located zero is built.
