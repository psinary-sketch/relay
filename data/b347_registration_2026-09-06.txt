### ### **THE DEFECT:** the gate flattener strips ONE leading `###` per line, so a sentence continued onto

### ### **THE FORM:** one NEW shared utility, `tools/gate_text.py`, carrying `flat` in its repaired form,

### matches under both. ### **THE OLD BEHAVIOUR IS KEPT IN THE UTILITY AS A NAMED FUNCTION SO THE FIXTURE
### ### CAN DISCRIMINATE, NOT SO THAT ANYTHING MAY CALL IT.**

### reads that act's own checks file, extracts STATICALLY every string literal compared against the
### flattened bank, and evaluates each under BOTH behaviours against that act's own bank. ### An arm whose

### ### WEAKENED**; it is named with its act and its phrase.
### ### ### **AND WHAT FOLLOWS FROM A WEAKENED ARM IS NOTHING AUTOMATIC.** ### An arm that failed to match

### (F) THE BAR-FLOOR RULE, OVER BOTH SPECIES, AND ITS GATE. ### [SEAT, PRE-RUN]
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### ### ### **THE RULE, AS THIS ACT MINTS IT:** ### **A NUMERICAL BAR IS STATED WITH THE FLOOR OF THE
### ### ### OBJECT IT TESTS. ### A BAR BELOW THAT FLOOR IS UNINFORMATIVE RATHER THAN STRICT. ### AND A BAR
### ### ### WITH SEVERAL ARMS IS STATED WITH WHAT MAKES THE ARMS INDEPENDENT; ARMS THAT ARE ALGEBRAICALLY
### ### ### ONE ARM ARE ONE ARM.**
### ### **IT IS THE RESOLVING-POWER RULE AT THE FIXTURE LAYER, AND THE MODULE SAYS SO.** ### b322's rule
### asks whether the candidates sit closer than the instrument's distance from the answer; this one asks
### the same question of a BAR and its OBJECT, one level down.
### ### **THE TWO INCIDENTS, BOTH CARRIED IN THE MODULE, EACH QUOTED FROM ITS OWN BANK:**
###   ### **b345** -- a fixture demanding `1e-25` of a routine whose sealed truncation left it a floor near
###     `4.4e-18`, so at its own threshold it rejected the CORRECT copy as well as the broken one and
###     separated nothing.
###   ### **b346** -- an uncertainty bar with three arms of which two could not contribute: a two-point
###     drift-zero is algebraically the local slope of those two points, so the second estimator collapsed
###     onto the first and the arm keyed to the window's bottom was structurally zero.
### ### **THE GATE, IN `tools/registration_gate.py`, ADDED BESIDE THE INDEX-QUERY ARM AND NOT REPLACING
### ### IT:**
###   ### **ARM ONE -- THE FLOOR ARM.** ### A line that states a numerical threshold must carry, within its
###     own paragraph, either the floor of the object it tests or the token `UNPRICED`.
###   ### **ARM TWO -- THE ARMS ARM.** ### A passage that declares a bar with several arms must carry,
###     within its own paragraph, either what makes the arms independent or the token `SINGLE-ARM`.
### ### **FIXTURES BOTH POLARITIES FOR EACH ARM:** a passage that satisfies it passes and a passage that
### does not FAILS, on synthetic text this act writes, so each arm is seen to discriminate before it is
### trusted.
### ### **AND THE GATE'S OWN REACH, IN ITS OWN HEADER, IN THE VOICE THE FILE ALREADY USES:** ### **IT
### ### MATCHES TEXT.** ### It cannot tell a floor from the words of one; a registration that writes
### `UNPRICED` beside every bar passes and has priced nothing. ### **IT RAISES THE COST OF AN UNPRICED BAR
### ### FROM ZERO TO A DELIBERATE WORD.**
### ### **THE CENSUS, BECAUSE A NEW GATE OVER AN OLD RECORD MUST BE MEASURED BEFORE IT IS BELIEVED:** both
### arms are run over every registration in `data/`, and the counts are reported. ### **NO PAST
### ### REGISTRATION IS RE-VERDICTED, NOTHING IS EDITED, AND A REGISTRATION THAT WOULD FIRE IS NAMED AND
### ### LEFT EXACTLY AS IT IS.** ### The gate binds registrations written AFTER it.
### ### **THIS ACT'S OWN REGISTRATION IS SUBJECT TO IT** -- the gate is run against this file too, and its
### verdict here is reported whatever it is.
