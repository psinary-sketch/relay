### ### STEP 1 -- THE INTERPOLATION ERROR, WHICH IS THE ONLY ERROR b238 LEFT STANDING.
### `np.interp` is piecewise LINEAR. ### The classical bound on a cell of width `h` is
###   ### **|f(x) - interp(x)| <= (h^2/8) * max |f''| over that cell**, and it is SHARP.
### ### THE CONSTANT `h^2/8` IS THE FERRY'S, AND IT IS NOT A CHOICE OF THIS ACT.

### ### STEP 2 -- `corr''` FROM THE BUMP'S DEFINITION, WITH THE CELL SCALED OUT.
### Write `phi(t) = exp(-1/(1-t^2))` on `|t| < 1` and `C = INT_{-1}^{1} phi`. ### Then
###   `w(v) = phi(v/L) / (L*C)`   -- and `INT w dv = 1` exactly, by the normalization.
### Substituting into the convolution and changing variables `s = L*sigma`:
###   `corr(y) = INT w(s) w(y-s) ds = (1/(L*C^2)) * PHI(y/L)`,  where
###   ### **`PHI(tau) := (phi * phi)(tau)`, a UNIVERSAL function on `|tau| <= 2`, independent of
###   ### the cell, of `a`, of `L` and of `NV`.**
### Differentiating twice:
###   ### **`corr''(y) = PHI''(y/L) / (L^3 * C^2)`,  with `PHI'' = phi * phi''`.**
### ### THAT IS THE WHOLE ANALYTIC ROUTE: ### ONE universal function computed ONCE, and every
### ### cell's `corr''` obtained from it by a scale factor. ### **NO MAXIMUM OVER THE
### ### INSTRUMENT'S OWN `corr` SAMPLES APPEARS ANYWHERE IN IT.**

### ### STEP 3 -- THE ENVELOPE ON `PR`.
### `PR = SUM_j c_j * corr(x_j)` with `c_j = 2 log p / p^(k/2) > 0` and `x_j = log p^k`. So
###   ### **|dPR| <= (h^2/8) * SUM_j c_j * max{ |corr''| on the cell containing x_j }.**
### TWO ENVELOPES ARE DERIVED AND BOTH ARE BANKED, THE CRUDER ONE GOVERNING:
###   ### **(E-glob)** `K_glob(a^2) = ||PHI''||_inf * W(a) / (8 * L^3 * C^2)`,
###        `W(a) := SUM_j c_j`. ### The global maximum at every point -- ### THE SAFEST AND THE
###        LOOSEST, and ### **THIS IS THE ONE THE CRITERION USES.**
###   ### **(E-pt)** the same sum with `||PHI''||_inf` replaced by the LOCAL maximum of `|PHI''|`
###        over `[x_j/L - h/L, x_j/L + h/L]`. ### Sharper, still analytic, still free of any
###        residual. ### **REPORTED, NOT USED FOR THE VERDICT** -- because a sharper bound that
###        happens to be the one that passes is the shape of the thing this act exists to avoid.

### ### STEP 4 -- WHAT THE ANALYTIC ROUTE ASSUMES, NAMED IN ADVANCE AND TESTED, NOT WAVED THROUGH.
### ###  (A-1) that `corr` AT THE NODES is the continuous `w*w` at those nodes to well below the
### ###        interpolation error. ### WARRANT: b238's S2 measured the trapezoid on this very
### ###        bump against `mpmath.quad` and got ### `|error| = 0.000e+00 AT EVERY N` -- the bump
### ###        is `C^inf` with every derivative vanishing at `t = +-1`, so Euler-Maclaurin has no
### ###        boundary terms at any order. ### **THIS ACT RE-RUNS THAT CONTROL RATHER THAN
### ###        CITING IT**, because the whole envelope rests on it.
### ###  (A-2) that `||PHI''||_inf` is computed to sufficient accuracy. ### TESTED BY SHOWING THE
### ###        MAXIMUM'S STABILITY ACROSS RESOLUTIONS -- the ferry's own requirement for the
### ###        sampled route, applied here to the universal function so the analytic route does
### ###        not escape the test by being analytic.
### ###  (A-3) that `W(a)` counts exactly the terms the instrument sums. ### TESTED against
