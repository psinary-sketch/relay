### (A) WHAT THIS ACT IS.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### ### **THE CORPUS HOLDS NO ERROR BOUND FOR THE PLACES-SIDE QUADRATURE** (b484`s filing, quoted).
### This act builds one, COMPUTED and not assumed, tests it against what the record has already
### measured, and recomputes each cell`s floor under it. ### **THIS ACT CONFERS NO GRADE**, and no
### number it prints is a statement about RH.
### ### **AN ERROR ESTIMATE IS NOT A PROOF.** ### A Richardson estimate assumes the error behaves
### like a power of the grid step; where the three levels do not show that behaviour the estimate
### is flagged NON-ASYMPTOTIC and made conservative by the rule in (B)(2), and the flag is printed.

### (1) THE INSTRUMENT AS IT STANDS. ### Quoted at its lines: `b321_window.channels` -- A is a
###     trapezoid over the u-grid `linspace(-UMAX, UMAX, NU)` (UMAX 600, NU 12001, `carto_atlas``s
###     constants) of `hhat(u) * kernel(u) / 2 pi`, and `hhat` is `cos(u v) @ (w dv)` on the v-grid;
###     `prime_sum` takes each term by `np.interp` of `w` at `log n` on the same v-grid; the v-grid is
###     `b318_square.autocorrelation``s, of `2 nv - 1` points at `nv = 8193`. ### What `trunc_bound`
###     bounds and what it does not, one sentence each.
### (2) THE BOUND. ### ### **STATED HERE, BEFORE IT IS RUN.** ### The instrument admits a grid
###     parameter, not a second rule, so the method is ### **RICHARDSON ACROSS THREE LEVELS** of the
###     v-grid, together with ### **A u-GRID REFINEMENT** of A`s integral. ### Per cell:
###     ### (v) the margin `m = A - PR` at `nv` = 8193, 16385, 32769 -- values `x1, x2, x3`;
###         `d1 = x1 - x2`, `d2 = x2 - x3`. ### If `d1` and `d2` share a sign and `|d1| > |d2| > 0`,
###         `r = |d1|/|d2|`, the extrapolate `xR = x3 - d2/(r - 1)`, and `E_v = |x1 - xR|`. ###
###         Otherwise the cell is ### **NON-ASYMPTOTIC** and `E_v = 2(|d1| + |d2|)`. ### If both are
###         exactly zero, `E_v = 0` and the cell says so.
###     ### (u) A at the base v-grid on three u-grids: the base (UMAX 600, NU 12001); the spacing
###         halved (NU 24001); the range doubled at the base spacing (UMAX 1200, NU 24001).
###         `E_u = |A_half - A_base| + |A_wide - A_base|`.
###     ### ### **THE BOUND IS `B = E_v + E_u`**, per cell, on the base value the chain reports.
###     ### The kernel cache of `carto_atlas` holds ONE u-grid; it is RESET before every change of
###     u-grid and the cached length is checked against the grid, because a stale cache would pair
###     the kernel of one grid with another grid and the program would not complain.
###     ### ### **WHAT THE BOUND DOES NOT COVER, NAMED:** the zero side`s truncation (that is
###     `trunc_bound``s), and the seed`s own nodes, which DEFINE the piecewise-linear test function
###     rather than approximate one.
### (3) THE TEST, ON THE THIRTY-FIVE BANKED CELLS. ### At each cell, `B` beside the achieved two-side
###     agreement `|W + Z|` from `b492_cells.json` -- the per-cell quantity whose spectral norm over
###     the ladder b483 printed as 3.169e-06. ### **THE ORDER`S TEST: `B >= |W + Z|` AT EVERY CELL**,
###     printed cell by cell and counted. ### Beside it, and not in place of it, the composite `B +
###     trunc_bound` against the same `|W + Z|`.
###     ### ### **POSITIVE CONTROL -- A CELL WITH A KNOWN VALUE:** a Gaussian `w(v) = exp(-v^2/2s^2)`
###     on `[-L, L]`, `L = 2.2`, `s = L/8`, on the chain`s grids at the three level sizes. Its
###     transform is CLOSED FORM, `hhat(u) = s sqrt(2 pi) exp(-s^2 u^2 / 2)`; its prime sum is exact
###     from `w` at `log n` without interpolation; A`s reference is the closed-form `hhat` against the
###     kernel by `mpmath.quad` at 30 digits over the whole line. ### The control PASSES if `B >= |m
###     - m_ref|` at the base level. ### ### **THE A-REFERENCE IS AN ADAPTIVE INTEGRAL OF A CLOSED
###     FORM, NOT A CLOSED FORM**, and the face says so rather than the report.
###     ### **NEGATIVE CONTROL -- THE GRID DELIBERATELY COARSENED:** at `a` = 1.3, 4.061553 and
###     5.656854 the bound recomputed with levels `nv` = 2049, 4097, 8193; it must be LARGER than
###     the base bound at each of the three.
### (4) THE FLOOR. ### Each cell`s new floor is its `B`, banked beside b477`s uniform floor 1.49e-08;
###     the count of cells whose margin `m(a)` exceeds its new floor.
