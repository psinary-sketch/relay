### (A) THE ORDER, AND WHAT THIS ACT IS.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### The order: ### **"ACT b368 -- THE FRONT DOCUMENT RECONCILED, OR PRICED. The executor's draft is
### ADOPTED, with four additions."**
### ### **THE ACT IS A RE-MEASUREMENT, A CLASSIFICATION, AND THEN ONE OF TWO BRANCHES.** ### It re-derives
### the absent set from the kernel itself at a pinned ref; classifies every absent name into one of four
### kinds with its evidence; and then either writes an ### **APPEND-ONLY** ### currency block or prices
### the repair and routes it to the author.
### ### **WHAT THIS ACT IS NOT.** ### **IN EITHER BRANCH THE KERNEL'S `.lean` FILES ARE NOT TOUCHED**, the
### order's own clause. ### No terminal is written, no statement is proved, no build is run, and no
### existing sentence of the front document is edited.

### (C) ADDITION TWO -- EACH NAME CLASSIFIED, ONE ROW EACH. ### **NEVER FROM ITS OWN SOUND.**
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
### ### **FOUR KINDS, AND EACH CARRIES ITS OWN EVIDENCE:**
###   ### **`PRESENT`** -- with its statement as the file has it, and ### **ITS AXIOM PROFILE READ FROM A
###     PRINTED PROFILE.** ### If no printed profile covers it, the row says ### **`NO PRINTED PROFILE`**
###     ### and no compile is run in its place.
###   ### **`RETIRED`** -- with ### **THE RETIREMENT LEDGER'S OWN LINE QUOTED.**
###   ### **`RENAMED`** -- with the successor ### **NAMED AND LOCATED**, at a file and by a declaration.
###   ### **`NEVER EXISTED`** -- ### **WITH THE SEARCH RECORDED**: what was searched for, where, and what
###     came back.
### ### ### **AND THE ORDER'S BAR, WHICH IS THE ONE THIS SEAT IS MOST ABLE TO BREAK: `NO NAME IS
### ### ### CLASSIFIED FROM ITS OWN SOUND`.** ### A name that looks like a successor of another is not a
### successor; a name that sounds retired is not retired. ### **A `RENAMED` ROW REQUIRES A LOCATED
### ### SUCCESSOR, AND A ROW THAT CANNOT PRODUCE ONE IS `NEVER EXISTED` OR `RETIRED`, NOT `RENAMED`.**
### ### **AND WHERE THE EVIDENCE FOR A KIND IS ABSENT, THE ROW SAYS SO AND TAKES THE WEAKER KIND.**
