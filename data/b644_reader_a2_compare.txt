### QUESTION 1: What is a hinge, and which premises are hinges?
    the reader: A hinge is a premise whose consumers sit in more than one kernel, or in more than one "chain" inside a single kernel. A consumer is a theorem whose statement or proof uses the premise. A chain is a connected group of the declarations that use the premise, directly or indirectly, in a kernel's use-graph. The document gives this definition in two versions. In v0.7 (2026-10-08), every premise with consumers spread like this counted, and there were 28 hinges. The refined definition in force from 2026-10-09 (ruling R254(4), used for v0.7.1) adds two conditions. First, the premise's "own evidence" is removed from the use-graph before the chains are counted. Own evidence means its Salt witnesses, any refutation concluding its negation, any declaration concluding the premise itself (bare or under other hypotheses), and its projections and constructors. Second, only premises with one of the five programme statuses (OPEN, CITED, DISCHARGED, WITNESSED, REFUTED-BY-COMPUTATION) can be hinges. A DOMAIN premise, meaning a Mathlib predicate that restricts a quantified variable, is printed with its chains but is not a hinge. Under the refined definition the v0.7.1 table lists 14 hinges: - EpsteinPremises - EulerFactorPremise - HCount - IdempotentAdd - NotDiv - Register4_channelInequality - Register4_positivity - SatisfiesCWeil - SelfDualFE - StepsI - StepsMI - TrivialSummandPremise - ZeroActingPairing - ZetaSeam All 14 are hinges because they have more than one chain inside a single kernel. None qualifies by spanning several kernels. Compared with v0.7, 16 premises left the list: - Ten DOMAIN heads: AnalyticOnNhd, Continuous, EqOn, HasCompactSupport, HasDerivAt, Integrable, IsRoot, Prime, StrictMono, Tendsto. Prime was v0.7's only hinge that spanned several kernels. - Six heads left with a single chain once their own evidence was removed: DealignedAt, IsExpansion, IsTrivialPoint, PWSetup, PlattTrudgianHeight, farSmall. Two premises joined the list: StepsMI and ZetaSeam. Removing their evidence split their single chain into two. The count checks: 28 − 16 + 2 = 14. The text says the only heads affected are DOMAIN heads (IsOpen, Monotone, Prime), so this cannot change the hinge list.
