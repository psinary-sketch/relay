b718 -- THE TOUCH READ'S TOOL, PRICED AND NOT BUILT ((R328)(3)): "A tool doing this read for every act is priced in data/b718_touch_price.txt and not built." (2026-10-28)

## What the tool would do

From the act's FINDINGS entry as the record tool composes it, before the root:
(1) list the objects the entry names, by the four kinds of (R328)(3) -- a declaration, a discriminant, an index, a figure -- each with its needle;
(2) look each needle up in FINDINGS.md, the census at its current version, FACES_LEDGER.md and the day papers (day1/*.md less the *_vN_N.md snapshots);
(3) bank every line found with its source, line and needle, its yield by source, and the control of every screen (the lines a screen leaves out);
(4) read each line against the entry and mark it AGREES or DIFFERS, printing each DIFFERS for the author.

## What this act's hand read took (relay data/b718_touch.txt)

18 needles (3 declarations, 5 discriminants, 1 index, 9 figures); 413 lines found, 402 AGREES and 11 DIFFERS. One screen built and controlled:
the discriminant needle (no digit, letter, dot, colon, slash or dash before; no digit, letter or dot after), its left-out lines read in full for -23,
-15, -39 and -47 (74 lines, every one a date, an exponent, a range or an id) and 12 of 294 for -20. The marking was read line by line; 192 of the
413 lines are one declaration, h2_sign_iff_rh, 125 of them in A_Place_to_Stand.md's erratum and ceiling-correction tables.

## The price

Steps (1)-(3), mechanical: one module of about 150 lines beside tools/b718_reads.py's locate, its needle list drawn from the entry's composer
(the figures the composer interpolates, tools/b718_record.py _figs, and the declarations and discriminants named in its text), the screens with their
controls, a planted line per needle that must be found and a planted date that the discriminant screen must leave out. One act, its controls
printed before its yield.
Step (4), the reading: not mechanical. A line found agrees or differs with the entry by what it says of the object, not by holding the needle. A
tool can pre-sort two classes -- lines that state the same figure beside the same object (AGREES by equality) and lines whose status word for the
object differs from the entry's (assumed beside derived, open beside proved, a cite to another clause) -- and leave every other line to the reader.
On this act's 413 lines the second class is the 11 DIFFERS; the first would pre-mark perhaps a third; the rest stay a hand read.
The tool is therefore priced as steps (1)-(3) built, with the pre-sort of (4) as a second module and the hand read kept, at one act for the first and
one for the second, each with its controls; the ruling's word decides whether either is built.
